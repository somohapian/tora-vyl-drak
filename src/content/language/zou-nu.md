<span class="kt">zou-nu</span>. new-goer: a first-year at <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/mara-skol">mara-skol</a>, roughly one hundred forty invited each year from the top of the skol cohort. most will not finish. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kau-esh">kau-esh</a> are what those who leave the path become, and the order maintains that both words are honorable, and mostly means it.
