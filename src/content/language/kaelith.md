kaelith is the kahu name for the bonded cat. the animals are descended from serval-cougar hybrids that arrived with the cat-farmers on <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mara">mara</a>'s founding diplomatic mission, and they have been bred and held ever since by <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-cat-kahu">the cat-kahu</a>. the name is used for the cat as a kind. an individual kaelith is known by its own name, and in the order's archives a bonded pair is recorded under both names together, the human and the kaelith.

the kaelith is not a pet. it is a working partner, bonded chiefly to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> but placed across <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> life wherever a single cat can hold a position: pest control in the granaries, livestock guardianship in the agricultural belt, solitary perimeter watch. the cat-kahu breed them, raise them, assess their readiness, and oversee the bonding. the deepest field work of the zou-esh depends on the kaelith, and the kaelith depend on the cat-kahu, so the chain of the order's most visible work runs back through a quiet agricultural specialist most kahu never think about.

the kaelith are long-lived. under kahu biotech support a kaelith's lifespan runs well beyond the cougar baseline, long enough that a single cat may know two human partners across a long life, and long enough that a zou-esh may bond with one kaelith through training and active service and a second through the long second half of life after retirement. the cat that retires beside a zou-esh is often not the cat that trained with them.

the kaelith are also peli-shen tolerant in a way no other kahu animal is. the cat-kahu have bred resistance into the lineage across the thousand years since the partnership began, and they administer a pre-mission inoculation, the kaelith's parallel to the zou-esh <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/air-klin">air-klin</a></span>, before a deep descent below the spore line. the cat that walks the lowlands beside a zou-esh has been prepared for it by the cat-kahu.

much about the kaelith is held for now as open canon. whether the cats carry any of the more dramatic traits sometimes attributed to them, and what ritual a scout undertakes when a bonded kaelith dies, are not yet settled. what is canon is the name, the lineage, the bond, and the length of the life.
