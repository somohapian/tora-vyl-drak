<span class="kt">toska</span> is the kahu-tok word for deep sorrow, grief, and anguished longing. it is a russian word, adopted into kahu-tok without change and included in <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-bixel-1000">the bixel 1000</a> as it stood. it is one of the few entries in the engineered core taken whole from an old-world language rather than built from roots. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bixel">bixel</a> included it after the death of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/akika">akika</a>, her partner. the word anchors the deep-grief vocabulary of <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/kahu-tok">kahu-tok</a>.
