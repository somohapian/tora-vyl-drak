<span class="kt">ol-hart</span>. old-heart: the vital things of the world before, brought home. the search half of <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-dual-mandate">the dual mandate</a> names its salvage this way, and the word carries the order's theology of scavenging: what the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> carry up <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-long-climb">the long climb</a> is not junk and not treasure. it is the old heart, still beating, put back into the body of the living.
