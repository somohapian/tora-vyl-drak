<span class="kt">konsil</span>: the seated body that decides; kept from the old tongue's council, respelled to the script. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bixel">bixel</a> kept <span class="kt">jefe</span> for the one who leads and <span class="kt">konsil</span> for the many who decide, and the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> have distrusted the first and trusted the second ever since.

the root carries the kahu's politics in miniature. <span class="kt">jefe</span> survives in the language chiefly in <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/jefe-tri">jefe-tri</a></span>, the name of a weapon, because the weapon, unlike the people, is permitted a boss. <span class="kt">konsil</span> survives in the name of the government itself: the <span class="kt">tapi-konsil</span>, the woven council, the formal body the record calls <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-council-of-the-tapestry">the council of the tapestry</a>.
