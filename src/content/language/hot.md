<span class="kt">hot</span> is the kahu-tok word for warm or heated. it is one of the foundational roots of the bixel 1000.

the word's direct inheritance from english is unmistakable. the bunker speech of the dark century preserved many basic english adjectives, and <span class="kt">hot</span> is among the words that came through to kahu-tok with its original form essentially intact. bixel's design did not require renaming everything from scratch; transparent inheritance where it served the language's needs was allowed to stand.

the most prominent compound is <span class="proper">hot-kasa</span> (warm-house, the warmth), the principal kahu settlement. the importance-first construction places the warmth first, the house second; what is being named is the warmth that the settlement provides, with the settlement itself as the qualifier. the word ordering reflects the kahu's actual relationship to the place: <span class="proper">hot-kasa</span> is, fundamentally, the warmth that the residual heat of the volcano provides. the chambers are the form that warmth takes.

the precise semantic range of <span class="kt">hot</span> in modern kahu-tok is open canon. whether it covers both the comfortable warmth of a chamber heated by the geothermal grid and the uncomfortable heat of a vent that needs handling care, or whether the language has separate roots for the two, is not yet settled in the archive.
