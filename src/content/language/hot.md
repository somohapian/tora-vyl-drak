<span class="kt">hot</span> is the kahu-tok word for warm or heated. it is one of the foundational roots of the bixel 1000.

the word is inherited directly from english. the bunker speech of the dark century preserved many basic english adjectives, and <span class="kt">hot</span> is among the words that came through to kahu-tok with its original form essentially intact.

the most prominent compound is <span class="proper">hot-kasa</span> (warm-house, the warmth), the principal kahu settlement. the importance-first construction places the warmth first, the house second. the warmth of <span class="proper">hot-kasa</span> derives from the residual heat of the volcano.

the precise semantic range of <span class="kt">hot</span> in modern kahu-tok is open canon. whether it covers both the comfortable warmth of a chamber heated by the geothermal grid and the uncomfortable heat of a vent that needs handling care, or whether the language has separate roots for the two, is not yet settled in the archive.
