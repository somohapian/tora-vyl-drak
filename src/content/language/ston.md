<span class="kt">ston</span> is the kahu-tok word for rock or stone. it is one of the foundational roots of the bixel 1000, and one of the most productive compounding roots in the language.

the word appears in many central kahu compounds. <span class="kt">ston-shen</span> (rock-life), the chemosynthetic lichen-bacteria paste that sustained the bunker survivors of the dark century. <span class="kt">ston-ropa</span> (rock-clothes), the shear-thickening cloth that forms zou-esh armor. <span class="proper">ston-klimb</span> (stone-climb), the eight-hundred-foot spiral walkway connecting the nu-tera plateau to the caldera floor. <span class="proper">hart-ston</span> (heart-stone), the sacred volcanic cone within the caldera. these compounds form a substantial portion of the working vocabulary of kahu-tok.

the word's apparent english cognate (stone) is noticeable. whether this reflects direct bunker-tongue inheritance through tok-peles, bixel's deliberate adoption of a transparent root, or independent coinage, is open canon. <span class="kt">ston</span> is one of the kahu-tok roots most easily recognized by english-speaking readers of the archive, which the kahu's archivists treat as a feature rather than a problem; transparency where it exists naturally is allowed to remain.

the personal name ston, attached to a figure in the kahu record whose details are still incomplete, may or may not relate etymologically to the kahu-tok word for rock. the relationship is open canon.
