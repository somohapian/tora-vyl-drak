<span class="kt">sun</span> is the kahu-tok word for the sun. it is one of the foundational roots of the bixel 1000, carrying direct english inheritance through the bunker speech of the dark century.

the word's most prominent compound is <span class="proper">sun-tera</span> (sun-land), the south quadrant of the caldera, the principal agricultural zone that receives the most direct sunlight available within the basin. the importance-first construction places the sunlight first; the land that receives it is named second.

direct sunlight is a partly-rationed resource for the kahu. the caldera rim shadows the basin on three sides for portions of each day, so not every part of the caldera floor gets the same access. <span class="proper">sun-tera</span> receives the most and carries out the basin's agricultural work.

whether <span class="kt">sun</span> distinguishes between the celestial body and the quality of sunlight is open canon. context disambiguates in most cases. the full inventory of <span class="kt">sun</span> compounds beyond <span class="proper">sun-tera</span> is open canon.
