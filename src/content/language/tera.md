<span class="kt">tera</span> is the kahu-tok word for land, ground, or earth. it is one of the foundational roots of the bixel 1000.

the romance-language inheritance (latin "terra," spanish/portuguese "tierra/terra," italian "terra") is unmistakable. bunker survivors of romance-language backgrounds preserved the root through the dark century, and bixel kept it intact in the engineered language. <span class="kt">tera</span> sits alongside <span class="kt">kasa</span>, <span class="kt">micasu</span>, and other words in the kahu-tok lexicon that show the romance-language thread running through the kahu inheritance.

<span class="kt">tera</span> appears in two of the central place names of kahu geography. <span class="proper">sun-tera</span> (sun-land) names the south quadrant of the caldera, the principal agricultural zone that receives the most direct sunlight available within the basin. <span class="proper">nu-tera</span> (new-land) names the agricultural plateau wrapping the southern flank of the caldera, the place where newcomers from below the spore line begin their kahu life. both compounds use the importance-first construction; the modifier (sun, new) leads, and <span class="kt">tera</span> follows.

whether <span class="kt">tera</span> covers the abstract sense of "earth" (the whole world, the planet) is open canon. the practical kahu usage covers land in the local sense; the broader sense may exist in formal or philosophical kahu-tok but is not well-attested in the working archive.
