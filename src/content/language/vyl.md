<span class="kt">vyl</span> carries two senses the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> decline to separate: the call, and the life-force that answers it. in the place name <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span> it is the call; in <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/language/kia-vyl">kia-vyl</a></span>, live, it is the living. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/mil-tok">mil-tok</a> glosses it as one word with one heart: to live is to answer the call. the deep i in its vowel is the last surviving sound of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bixel">bixel</a>'s mother tongue.

the unified sense is why the compounds read the way they do. <span class="kt">vyl-tapi</span>, the kahu-tok term for biotech, is the living weave, the life-force put to work. the inscription <span class="kt">!kia-vyl</span>, mara's last command, uses <span class="kt">vyl</span> as the verb root in the imperative construction "live!"
