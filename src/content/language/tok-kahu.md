word: tok-kahu

part_of_speech: noun
definition: the family of words. the archive's dictionary of kahu-tok, in which every word of the language holds its own entry.
etymology: compound of tok (speech, word) and kahu (family, community), the mirror of kahu-tok. the community's speech, turned to face the speech's community.
source: coined by the present archivist for the archive's dictionary
entered: 4625, the age of endura
related:

recorded: 4625, the present endura
open_canon:
  - whether the kahu themselves keep a spoken word for the dictionary as an institution, or whether tok-kahu remains the archive's term
---

<span class="kt">tok-kahu</span> is the archive's name for its own dictionary: the family of words. where <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/language/kahu-tok">kahu-tok</a></span> names the community's speech, the mirror compound names the speech's community, every word a member, gathered in one place. <a class="wiki-link" href="/dictionary">the dictionary itself lives here</a>.

the distinction between the tok-kahu and <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/mil-tok">the mil-tok</a> is the distinction the archive keeps everywhere: <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bixel">bixel</a>'s mil-tok is a primary document, in the voice of its maker, and stays whole; the tok-kahu is the archive speaking, and grows as the record grows. a word enters the tok-kahu when the record attests it. bixel's words carry her glosses; the words the centuries added carry the record's.
