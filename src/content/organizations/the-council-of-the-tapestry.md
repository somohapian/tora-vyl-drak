the council of the tapestry is the formal structure of <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> governance. where <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-council">the council</a> is the everyday name for the integrating body that deliberates on caldera-wide questions, the council of the tapestry is its formal shape: a bicameral assembly with two chambers, an executive triad, and a separate judicial body.

the upper chamber is the council of watchers. it is made up of retired <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a>, the order's members who have completed their active service and turned to the order's internal and advisory life. the watchers hold a veto on matters of long-term survival. theirs is the chamber of the long memory and the hard caution, staffed by people who have walked the lowlands and seen what the world outside the sanctuary has become. when the watchers veto, they are saying that a thing endangers the continued existence of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>, and that is the one ground on which their veto stands.

the lower chamber is the people's council. it is drawn from representatives of each major kahu and each profession, the working sub-communities and vocational groups of the caldera and the plateau. this is the chamber of the present, of daily life, of the broad range of kahu interests. it is the larger and more various body.

the structure expresses the kahu's politics. the people's council carries the will of the living community. the council of watchers carries the constraint of survival, the voice of those whose work is the threshold. neither chamber alone governs. the two are set against each other on purpose, the present and the long horizon held in tension, the way the whole kahu world holds abundance and fragility in tension at once.

executive work is carried by <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-three-speakers">the three speakers</a>, a triad that articulates the consensus of the two chambers. judicial work is carried by <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-pathfinders">the pathfinders</a>, a panel of three elders who hold the kahu's system of justice. the council of the tapestry sits in <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/hot-kasa">hot-kasa</a>.

the council of the tapestry's relationship to the hearthstone council, a body mentioned as overseeing the zou-esh, is open canon. whether they are the same body, related bodies, or distinct is not settled. so are the term lengths, the selection processes, and the precise line between what the two chambers each decide.
