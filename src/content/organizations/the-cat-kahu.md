the cat-kahu are the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> order responsible for the breeding, training, and stewardship of the kahu cats. they are a small lineage-based community living on the wrap-around of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/nu-tera">nu-tera</a> plateau, the northern shaded flank of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>'s rim. they are the active biological designers of the cat species and the partners-in-training to every <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> who works with a cat in the field.

the cat-kahu trace their continuity to the arrival of the cats and the cat-farmers with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mara">mara</a>'s diplomatic mission during the <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/age-of-bloom">age of bloom</a>. the precise lineage of the order from those founding arrivals to the present is open canon. what is settled is that for approximately a thousand years the cat-kahu have been a continuous community, working with the cats across generations, refining the species through deliberate breeding, and serving as the kahu institution that holds knowledge about the cats.

their work is biological. the serval-cougar hybrids that arrived with mara's mission have been continuously evolved under the cat-kahu's stewardship across a thousand years of kahu biotech support and selective breeding. adult cats now run notably larger than the cougar baseline. lifespans have been extended to match the long working life of a zou-esh; a single cat may know two human partners across its life. the resistance to <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/peli-shen"><span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span></a> that the kahu cats carry into the lowlands during zou-esh missions is the product of cat-kahu work, native resistance bred into the lineage over centuries combined with pre-mission inoculation that the cat-kahu administer before each deployment.

operationally, the cat-kahu pair cats with zou-esh students as part of the long training of the order. a zou-esh and a cat develop trust and trained sequence together during the years of training, deploy together across the active career, and the relationship ends only when one of them dies. the cat-kahu maintain the breeding programs that produce the cats, supply the cats to the order at the appropriate stage of student training, and provide ongoing veterinary and biological care across the working life of each cat. they do not, in general, train the cats for combat directly; that work happens in partnership between the cat, the zou-esh student, and the order's training staff at mara-skol.

the cat-kahu have specialized livestock operations on the wrap-around that supply meat to feed the cats. this is part of why the wrap-around is their territory; the meat economy of the cats requires land. the broader nu-tera agricultural belt is grain and vegetable; the wrap-around is hardy livestock for the cats.

the cat-kahu are kahu. they are one of the vocations through which the kahu live, responsible for the relationship between humans and the kahu cats.
