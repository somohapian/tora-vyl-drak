the <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> are the scholar-warrior order of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a>. their work has two parts. they defend <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-gate">the gate</a>, the chokepoint at the top of <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-long-climb">the long climb</a>, against any incursion from below. and they leave the sanctuary on long missions into the world the kahu have largely left behind, finding survivors, gathering supplies, recording what the lowlands have become, and bringing the worthy home.

these two faces are formalized as <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-dual-mandate">the dual mandate</a>: the search, the duty of finding survivors and scavenging vital resources, and the study, the scientific work of recording what the world outside has become. the warrior carries the search. the scholar carries the study. one zou-esh holds both.

the order is small. only approximately ten percent of kahu students are even asked to try for zou-esh. of those who are asked, only one or two percent make it through screening. net, roughly one or two students in a thousand enter actual zou-esh training. training takes a minimum of fifteen years and often longer. a large proportion of those who begin do not complete it. each year roughly the top tenth of kahu graduates are invited to attempt the path, and the attrition through training runs well above ninety percent. washing out of zou-esh training is not considered shameful. those still on the path and those who left it are the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kau-esh">kau-esh</a>, the little kahu, and they are scattered through kahu society in roles that benefit from the partial training and internal knowledge they carry.

the order's protocol in the field is **watch first**. a zou-esh will observe a target for days before initiating contact. the reason is operational. they will not lead anyone untrustworthy back to <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>. the judgment they make is final. zou-esh have full and unilateral authority to kill. if they judge a person a raider or otherwise dangerous, that person dies, without warning or any process of appeal.

if instead they judge a person worth bringing in, contact is initiated, the person is offered the path up to the caldera, and the zou-esh bring them above the spore line to the gate, where the quarantine and integration process begins. the zou-esh do not abandon people they have brought in. care during quarantine is part of the order's work.

the field operations of the zou-esh are made possible by their kit. the cloth mask filters <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/peli-shen"><span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span></a> at lowland concentrations. the bio-monitoring undersuit klin-soma tracks their bodies' state. the shear-thickening cloth ston-ropa, woven with an algae-derived component, looks like ordinary clothing and hardens to rock under impact. the passive camouflage cloak mirar-sin-ropa lets them disappear into terrain. the gloves adhere to their weapon. the jefe-tri bow, designed by each zou-esh as part of training, breaks apart through a personal trinity lock into a working close-quarters configuration of two blades, blade plus baton, or two batons.
partnering each zou-esh in the field is a kahu cat, bred by <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-cat-kahu">the cat-kahu</a> over a thousand years from the original serval-cougar hybrids that arrived with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mara">mara</a>'s mission. the cat is the zou-esh's partner across the working life of both, and beyond it. zou-esh and cat operate together by trust and by trained sequence. neither functions in the field as well alone.

the order's lifecycle is built around the seventy-five mission cap. training is complete at thirty-five to forty years of age. missions run only in summer, when the long climb is open; most missions are single-summer, some take a year or two. midlife retirement comes at the seventy-fifth mission. the cap is set one short of three from mara's seventy-eight. no living zou-esh ever exceeds her. the second half of a zou-esh life, post-active, is given to training the next cohort, staffing mara-skol, and the order's internal work. a typical zou-esh career runs from the mid-thirties to the second century of life under kahu biotech.

in the middle of the bloom, the order's head, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/edi">edi</a>, used its standing to seize control of the caldera and the plateau (see the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-seizure">seizure</a>). the zou-esh were not united behind him, and members of the order, among them the young mara, stood in the resistance that deposed and exiled him. this event is the reason the order is held in the present to its mandate and its limits with particular firmness, having once produced a direct threat to kahu self-government.

the zou-esh are kahu. they are not external to the kahu. they are one of the vocations through which the kahu live.
