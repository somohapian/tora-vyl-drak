the warden-tenders are the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> order responsible for the living systems of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>. they are an apothecary-priest hybrid. their work is upstream of every kahu decision that depends on biotech, ecological management, or the consultation of <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/warden">warden</a>.

the warden-tenders maintain the agent stack. the viral and bacterial systems that handle daily kahu hygiene, food optimization, disease suppression, and the longevity intervention that lets kahu live into their second century are all grown, maintained, and updated by the order. without the warden-tenders' continuous work, the agent stack would degrade, the kahu would lose their biotech inheritance over a generation, and the population at its current scale and density would become unsustainable. this is not theoretical. the order's importance is structural.

the warden-tenders also maintain warden itself. the biological supercomputer's substrate, its growth conditions, its sensors, its consultation interfaces, all require continuous tending. warden is alive; alive things require care. the order is what supplies that care. the interface by which <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-council">the council</a> consults warden runs through the warden-tenders' physical infrastructure in <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-hearth">the hearth</a>.

the warden-tenders staff the principal control nodes of the geothermal grid, the kahu's other large living-system, the engineered interface between the underlying volcano's residual heat and the chambers and infrastructure that depend on it. the grid runs hot but it requires watching. the order watches it.

medically, the warden-tenders are the kahu's frame of reference. individual kahu medical issues are usually handled by kahu of intermediate training, kahu-tok term open canon, with formal warden-tender consultation reserved for the cases that need it. but the standards by which medical practice in the caldera operates, the formulations of the agents that any kahu medical worker can apply, the protocols for unusual cases, all originate within the warden-tenders and flow outward.

the order has the apothecary-priest character because its work is simultaneously practical and meaning-bearing. the agents that the warden-tenders maintain are the kahu's relationship with mortality. how aggressively the agents are deployed in the late years of a kahu life is the substance of <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-schism">the schism</a>. the warden-tenders are not the schism's deciders, but they are its instruments. both factions of the disagreement work through the same order, and the order's internal practices reflect the strain.

the warden-tenders also hold the registry of names. every kahu name is unique among the living, and it is the warden-tenders who keep that true. when a child is born, the kahu brings it to the warden-tender within a few days, and the order tells the family which names are free and which are taken or retired, working through them until one answers. a newborn enters the archive within days of birth, and a name leaves the registry when its holder dies, to return to the pool or to rest or to be retired, according to the customs around <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-naming">the naming</a>. the registry is administered through warden, but it is the warden-tenders who administer it for the people: the order is the human face a kahu meets when it comes to name a child.

the warden-tenders are kahu. they are one of the vocations through which the kahu live. their work is the work of holding the conditions of life in place, every day, for every kahu, with the steady attention that the conditions require.
