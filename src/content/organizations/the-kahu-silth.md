the kahu-silth are the order of bio-artisans who grow, shape, and heal the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a>'s living garments. the name is built from <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/language/silth">silth</a></span>, the kahu-tok root for weaving and fiber, and <span class="kt">kahu</span>, the community. they are the weaving-kahu, though the work is closer to biology than to weaving as the old world understood it.

a kahu-silth is several things at once. a biologist, because the garments are living systems. a healer, because some of those garments are medical. a textile engineer, because the cloth must be structured to do its work. a microbiome cultivator, because the cloth's properties come from the bacteria and algae woven through it. and a ritual artisan, because some of what they make carries meaning beyond function. the vocation does not separate these roles. one kahu-silth holds all of them, the way a <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> holds both the scholar and the warrior.

their materials are alive. the kahu-silth work in the same <span class="kt">vyl-tapi</span> biotech tradition that produces kahu medicine and food, applied to fiber. human hair serves as structural thread. algae are integrated into the cloth for warmth, moisture handling, and pigment. bacteria are cultivated through the textile for self-cleaning and antimicrobial protection. viral vector systems give the cloth ecological stability and let it adapt to its wearer over time. a garment from the kahu-silth is not made once and worn until it fails. it is grown, and it goes on living against the skin of the person who wears it.

the order produces several kinds of garment. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/living-textiles">living textiles</a> entry covers them in detail: the bio-veil, the healing weave, the thermic sheath, and the braidcloth, a ceremonial garment woven in part from the hair of ancestors. the zou-esh kit overlaps with this tradition. the shear-thickening <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/ston-ropa">ston-ropa</a></span> that hardens to rock under impact, and the <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/mirar-sin-ropa">mirar-sin-ropa</a></span> camouflage cloak, are living textiles of the same kind, whether made by the kahu-silth directly or by zou-esh trained in the craft is open canon.

the kahu-silth are one of the vocations through which the kahu argument against the sovereign individual is made concrete. like the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-cat-kahu">cat-kahu</a>, they are a quiet specialist order whose work is upstream of the order everyone celebrates. the zou-esh cannot walk the lowlands without cloth that hardens to stone and a cloak that hides them. the kahu-silth make it. their contribution is mostly invisible from the outside, and the kahu know it is there.

the order's founding, its internal structure, and its full ceremonial place in kahu life are open canon.
