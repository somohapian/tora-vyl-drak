the council of six was the kahu's first government. it was formed around 2122 by the six main groups that reached the caldera in <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-first-descent">the first descent</a>: the chinese carrier, the cheyenne and government bunkers, the billionaire bunker, and the southern and northern caravans. each group held one seat, and the council was presided over by a single leader chosen from among them.

the body expanded over the centuries. about forty to fifty years after the founding the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> were established to record and classify the world beyond the rim; roughly a century later, having become indispensable, they were granted a seventh seat, and the body became the council of seven.

a standing tension ran under its whole life. the cheyenne and government bunkers, in substance the surviving united states state, held two of the seats between them, which the groups that were not that state resented and which was part of why those groups backed the zou-esh seat, to dilute it. the deeper and never-resolved fault ran between the caldera floor and the plateau, and it deadlocked the council repeatedly.

the council of six ran for roughly eleven hundred years, through the <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/age-of-tapestry">age of tapestry</a> and into the <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/age-of-bloom">age of bloom</a>. it ended around 3250, when <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/edi">edi</a>, the head of the zou-esh, captured its powers and dissolved the republic (see <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-seizure">the seizure</a>). the government that replaced it, built by the resistance that deposed edi, was the bicameral <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-council">council</a> of the present.
