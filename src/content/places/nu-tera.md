<span class="proper">nu-tera</span> is the plateau that wraps the southern flank of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>. the name in kahu-tok reads new-land. the plateau clears the <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span> spore line. nu-tera's fields sit above the kill line and inside the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-goldilocks-filter">goldilocks filter</a>, and life on the plateau is life in clean air. the danger begins one step past <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-gate">the gate</a>: <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-long-climb">the long climb</a> descends through the fade band for its whole length, and the safety of nu-tera ends exactly where the descent begins. nu-tera is fertile despite the altitude.

the plateau is approximately thirteen miles long and two to five miles wide along its southern stretch. a narrower wrap-around extends along the shaded northern flank. total plateau area is roughly seventy-eight square miles. the southern stretch is the principal agricultural belt and the main settlement area. the wrap-around is given over to <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-cat-kahu">the cat-kahu</a>, whose livestock operations supply meat for the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> cats.

approximately twenty-eight thousand kahu live in the southern agricultural belt. another three thousand five hundred or so live with the cat-kahu on the wrap-around. about fifteen hundred more are at mara-skol and <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-gate">the gate</a> at the eastern end of the plateau.

the plateau is not uniform. nearest the gate live the newest arrivals, quarantined on arrival and housed close to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> compound through the detox and the first seasons of integration. the belt beyond is the settled agricultural life of the plateau. movement through nu-tera, for those who move at all, is a gradient: arrive, quarantine, settle, and then choose. staying is not a failure to integrate. it is one of the two full forms of kahu life, and the plateau's farms are proof that the choice is honored.

a person who arrives at nu-tera gains several things. they have escaped the spore line and its slow neurotoxicity. they have escaped raiders. they gain reliable food from the plateau's agricultural surplus, and access to baseline kahu biotech, even at probationary status. an adult arriving at nu-tera roughly doubles their remaining life expectancy compared to staying below.

the standing tension at nu-tera concerns the timing of full caldera integration for those who want it. the complaint heard at nu-tera, "why do we have to stay down here and work fields," comes specifically from those who want to move on. the order treats the question as a test of the system's legitimacy: if not answered, the arrangement risks becoming a permanent caste structure.

the cat-farmer arrivals, who came with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mara">mara</a>'s diplomatic mission and brought the cats into kahu life, were quarantined at the gate and settled at nu-tera at the standard pace. they were not fast-tracked. their cats spread upward and outward through other carriers over generations. in many cases the cat lineage moved up to the caldera before the human lineage did. whether the cat-farmer descendants still exist as a recognizable group at nu-tera, and what they are called, is open canon.
