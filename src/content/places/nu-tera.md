<span class="proper">nu-tera</span> is the plateau that wraps the southern flank of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>. the name in kahu-tok reads new-land. the plateau sits just above five thousand feet, on the safe side of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/peli-shen"><span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span></a> spore line by a margin of dozens to hundreds of feet depending on the season and the year. the goldilocks filter, the same combination of altitude, temperature, and ultraviolet exposure that makes the caldera viable, applies to the plateau directly. nu-tera is fertile despite the altitude.

the plateau is approximately thirteen miles long and two to five miles wide along its southern stretch. a narrower wrap-around extends along the shaded northern flank. total plateau area is roughly seventy-eight square miles. the southern stretch is the principal agricultural belt and the main settlement area. the wrap-around is given over to <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-cat-kahu">the cat-kahu</a>, whose livestock operations supply meat for the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> cats.

approximately twenty-eight thousand kahu live in the southern agricultural belt. another three thousand five hundred or so live with the cat-kahu on the wrap-around. about fifteen hundred more are at mara-skol and <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-gate">the gate</a> at the eastern end of the plateau.

nu-tera is structured as a gradient. nearest the gate, near the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> compound, is the quarantine zone. newcomers arrive here under observation, recovering from peli-shen exposure and being assessed for integration. outward from the quarantine zone is the broader agricultural belt, where settled and more autonomous residents live their lives. less zou-esh oversight, more day-to-day kahu rhythm.

a person who arrives at nu-tera has already gained substantially. they have escaped the spore line and its slow neurotoxicity. they have escaped raiders. they have gained reliable food, the plateau's agricultural surplus. they have gained access to baseline kahu biotech, even at probationary status. an adult arriving at nu-tera roughly doubles their remaining life expectancy compared to staying below.

staying at nu-tera is a legitimate path, not a failure to integrate. a farmer who arrives a farmer and stays a farmer is making a complete life choice. the plateau is fertile, the work is meaningful, the community functions, and the threats below the gate do not reach. some who arrive at nu-tera move up to the caldera; some make a home on the plateau. both are valid forms of kahu life.

the standing tension at nu-tera is not about whether the plateau is a home. it is about the timing of full caldera integration for those who want it. the complaint heard at nu-tera, "why do we have to stay down here and work fields," comes specifically from those who want to move on. the question tests the legitimacy of the kahu system. the order has to answer it honestly or the system becomes a permanent caste arrangement dressed in better language.

the cat-farmer arrivals, who came with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mara">mara</a>'s diplomatic mission and brought the cats into kahu life, were quarantined at the gate and settled at nu-tera at the standard pace. they were not fast-tracked. their cats, however, spread upward and outward through other carriers over generations. in many cases the cat lineage moved up to the caldera before the human lineage did. whether the cat-farmer descendants still exist as a recognizable group at nu-tera, and what they are called, is open canon.
