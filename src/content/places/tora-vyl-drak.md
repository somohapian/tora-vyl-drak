<span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span> is the eighteen-mile volcanic caldera in <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-cascade">the cascade</a> range that has been the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> sanctuary since <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-first-descent">the first descent</a>, around 2120 ad. the name reads, in importance-first kahu-tok, hope, call, last: the hope of the last call.<sup class="cite"><a href="#src-1" id="ref-1">1</a></sup>

the caldera formed in 2075 in a vei-8 super-eruption of the cascade volcanic arc. the eruption was triggered roughly five weeks after the cascadia impact of that year destabilized the magma chambers beneath the arc. the explosion vaporized thousands of square miles of toxic surface soil, blew a dust column into the upper atmosphere, and collapsed inward to form a pristine, sterile, eighteen-mile basin. the floor sits at six thousand feet. the rim rises to between nine and eleven thousand feet, three to five thousand feet above the floor.<sup class="cite"><a href="#src-2" id="ref-2">2</a></sup>

the elevation is the first reason the kahu can live here. the spore line of <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span>, the fungus that has rendered the lowlands lethal, sits at approximately five thousand feet. the caldera floor is a thousand feet above the line, a narrow margin.

the geology is the second reason. the underlying volcano is mostly dead but slightly alive. its residual magma chamber powers the <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/hot-kasa">hot-kasa</a></span> thermal grid that heats the interior chambers carved through lava tubes and natural flow voids. the caldera does not freeze in winter. it does not erupt again either, except for the warning that became <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-caldera-shudder">the caldera shudder</a> of approximately 4525 ad.

the floor of the caldera totals roughly two hundred and four square miles of land surrounding <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/hart-wata">hart-wata</a>, the great lake at the center. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/sun-tera">sun-tera</a> quadrant to the south is the breadbasket. <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-hearth">the hearth</a> to the west holds the geothermal grid and the principal settlement, hot-kasa. <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-wilds">the wilds</a> to the north hold the <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/ol-tri-shen">ol-tri shen</a> forest preserve. the eastern approach is <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-threshold">the threshold</a>, the transitional quadrant where the stone climb arrives from the <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/nu-tera">nu-tera</a> plateau below.

approximately two hundred and forty-five thousand kahu live inside the caldera. another thirty thousand or so live on the nu-tera plateau just below the rim, primarily in the settled agricultural belt and at <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-gate">the gate</a>. the record's distribution runs: hot-kasa about one hundred twenty thousand; the distributed rim chambers and settlements about one hundred twenty-five thousand; nu-tera south about twenty-eight thousand; <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-wrap-around">the wrap-around</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-cat-kahu">the cat-kahu</a> about thirty-five hundred; <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/mara-skol">mara-skol</a> and the gate about fifteen hundred.

the kahu speak of the caldera as the place itself. outside the rim, in degraded folk reports from distant survivor pockets, the place became myth. distant communities in the russian far east called it <span class="proper">tura vyl drak</span>, mistranslating the kahu-tok roots through their own linguistic lens to arrive at "the last dragon's hope" or "the valley of the second sun." the descendants of the yamantau bunker undertook a multi-generational migration across the bering ice bridge to find the place on the basis of these stories. some arrived.<sup class="cite"><a href="#src-3" id="ref-3">3</a></sup>
