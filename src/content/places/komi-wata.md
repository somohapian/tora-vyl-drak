komi-wata is the river of <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/sun-tera">sun-tera</a>, the south quadrant of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>. the name carries <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/language/wata">wata</a></span>, the kahu-tok root for water. it is the working water of the breadbasket, the river the south quadrant's agriculture is built around.

sun-tera is the caldera's primary cropland, and komi-wata is what makes it productive. the river feeds the vyl-tapi algae terraces, the stepped cultivation beds where the kahu grow the algae that carries the bulk of their calories, and it waters the conventional croplands that supply the variety and the fresh food the algae cannot. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a>'s food economy is comfortable on calories and tight on diversity, and the diversity that exists grows in the sun and the water of the south quadrant, along komi-wata.

the river is one of the caldera's two great waters, set against <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/hart-wata">hart-wata</a>, the great lake at the basin's center. where hart-wata is the still water, the body that holds the aquaculture and anchors the geography, komi-wata is the moving water, the flow that does the agricultural work of the south. whether komi-wata drains into hart-wata or runs its own course is open canon, as is the literal meaning of the komi root and the full reach of the terraces it feeds.
