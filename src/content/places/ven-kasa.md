<span class="kt">ven-kasa</span>. the come-house: the arrival hall and quarantine houses nearest <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-gate">the gate</a> on <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/nu-tera">nu-tera</a>, where newcomers live their first seasons. the name is the doctrine. before a newcomer is asked anything, they are told where they are: a house built for their coming.
