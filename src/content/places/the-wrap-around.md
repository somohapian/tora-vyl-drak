the wrap-around is the northern shaded flank of the nu-tera plateau. it wraps the northern face of <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span>'s rim around to meet the broader plateau on its eastern and western edges. it is the cat-kahu's territory. roughly thirty-five hundred kahu live and work here, all in the cat-kahu lineage or its working community.

the wrap-around is geographically distinct from the southern agricultural belt of nu-tera. it is cooler, more exposed to north-facing weather, and less suited to the broad grain and vegetable cultivation that defines the southern belt. what it is well suited to is the hardier livestock operations the cat-kahu maintain to feed the kahu cats. the species are open canon, but the broad pattern is hardy mountain-tolerant herd animals that thrive on the wrap-around's more marginal terrain.

the cats themselves live throughout the wrap-around. the cat-kahu's breeding programs, training facilities, and veterinary operations are distributed across the territory rather than centralized in a single settlement. cats and their human partners (cat-kahu in training, cat-kahu working with zou-esh students, retired zou-esh whose cats live out their later years here) are visible across the landscape in a way they are not anywhere else in <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span>.

the wrap-around shares its rim with the wilds, the northern quadrant of the caldera floor above. the relationship between the two is collaborative. cat-kahu occasionally bring cats up into the wilds for breeding and training in less constrained conditions than the wrap-around allows. the precise frequency and ceremonial structure of this practice is open canon. the wilds are the place where some of the cats' most demanding training happens.

the wrap-around's role in the broader kahu economy is supplying the cats. without the wrap-around's livestock operations, the cat-kahu's breeding programs, and the institutional continuity the territory holds, the partnership between the kahu and the cats would not be sustainable. every kahu cat working alongside a zou-esh in the field below the spore line traces its life back through the wrap-around.
