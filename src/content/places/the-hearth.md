the hearth is the west quadrant of the caldera, the part of the basin nearest to the residual magma chamber beneath <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>. it holds the geothermal grid, the principal settlement of <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/hot-kasa">hot-kasa</a>, and most of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a>'s industrial and infrastructure systems.

the volcano beneath the caldera is mostly dead but slightly alive. its remaining heat reaches the surface most strongly in the west quadrant, where the chamber networks of the original lava tubes run deepest into the rock. the kahu built hot-kasa here, the dense three-dimensional network of carved chambers that houses approximately one hundred and twenty thousand people, because the rock here is warm.

the geothermal grid is a network of pipes, exchangers, and circulation systems that carries the heat of the rock outward through hot-kasa's chambers and, in a reduced form, to <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/sun-tera">sun-tera</a> for greenhouse cultivation and to <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-warden-tenders">the warden-tenders</a>' workspaces. the grid is the technological foundation of life in the caldera, and its maintenance is continuous. the warden-tenders staff its principal control nodes and its critical interfaces with the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/warden">warden</a> modeling system.

the hearth is also where most kahu industrial production happens: ceramics, metals from salvaged sources, biotech vessels for the agent stack, and textile work at scale. the work is decentralized rather than factory-organized, carried out in small workshops staffed by sub-communities, with the warden-tenders coordinating across them rather than directing.
