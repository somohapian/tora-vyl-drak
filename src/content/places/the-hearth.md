the hearth is the west quadrant of the caldera, the part of the basin nearest to the residual magma chamber beneath <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span>. it holds the geothermal grid, the principal settlement of hot-kasa, and most of the kahu's industrial and infrastructure systems.

the hearth is named for what it is. the volcano beneath the caldera is mostly dead but slightly alive. its remaining heat reaches the surface most strongly in the west quadrant, where the chamber networks of the original lava tubes run deepest into the rock. the kahu built hot-kasa here, the dense three-dimensional warren of carved chambers that houses approximately one hundred and twenty thousand people, because here is where the rock is warm.

the geothermal grid itself is a network of pipes, exchangers, and circulation systems that carries the heat of the rock outward through hot-kasa's chambers and, in a reduced form, to sun-tera for greenhouse cultivation and to the warden-tenders' workspaces. the grid is the technological foundation of life in the caldera. its maintenance is a continuous activity. the warden-tenders staff its principal control nodes and its critical interfaces with the warden modeling system.

the hearth is also where most kahu industrial production happens: ceramics, metals from salvaged sources, biotech vessels for the agent stack, textile work at scale. the work is decentralized rather than factory-organized; small workshops worked by sub-communities, with the warden-tenders coordinating across them rather than directing.
