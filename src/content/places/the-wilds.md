the wilds are the north quadrant of the caldera. the quadrant is mostly unsettled. it holds the ol-tri shen forest preserve and the kahu's principal contact with non-cultivated ecology inside the rim.

the wilds are not unused. they are uncultivated. the kahu maintain them as a deliberate counterweight to the heavy management of sun-tera and the heavy density of the hearth. some land inside <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span> is left to grow without instruction from the warden-tenders or the council. the wilds are that land.

the principal feature of the wilds is the ol-tri shen forest, the old-tree forest preserve that the kahu have allowed to mature continuously since the early generations of the age of tapestry. ol-tri shen is the closest thing to ancient ecology inside the caldera; nothing in the basin predates 2133 ad, but ol-tri shen approaches the closest possible approximation of forest left alone for the longest time.

the wilds are visited by kahu in small numbers and for specific purposes. observation of natural systems, training for the zou-esh, occasional retreat, recovery from intense work in the dense settlements. the wilds are not roads or hunting grounds. they are the kahu's deliberate practice of letting one part of their world go on without them.

the cat-kahu, whose principal territory is the nu-tera plateau wrap-around, occasionally bring kahu cats up into the wilds for breeding and training in less constrained conditions than the plateau allows. the precise nature and frequency of this practice is open canon.
