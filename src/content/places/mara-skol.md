<span class="proper">mara-skol</span> is the zou-esh training institution. the name in kahu-tok reads mara-school. the school is located at the gate, the chokepoint where the long climb meets the nu-tera plateau, the place where mara died.

the school's location is deliberate. mara died defending the gate, and modern zou-esh live and train at the gate. the school sits at the place where mara gave her last command and died. students at mara-skol pass the <span class="kt">!kia-vyl</span> inscription daily.

approximately fifteen hundred kahu live and work at mara-skol and the broader gate complex. the figure includes students in active training, instructors, retired zou-esh in their post-active second careers, and the support staff and families of all of the above. the school's actual student-instructor count is open canon, but the total community at the gate has been roughly stable across the recent eras.

training takes a minimum of fifteen years and often longer. the curriculum is open canon in specifics, but the structure runs from initial selection (roughly one or two students in a thousand kahu are invited to try) through a long period of foundational training that includes the trinity lock, the watch-first protocol, the kahu-tok deep formalisms, the cat partnership, the kit (with the jefe-tri bow designed by the student over the years of training), the formal weapons styles, the field operations protocols, and the kahu-tok ceremonial elements specific to the order. a large proportion of students do not complete training. washing out of mara-skol is not considered shameful, and the partial training is itself valuable; zou-esh-adjacent kahu carry their education into many roles across the caldera.

mara-skol also holds the institutional memory of the order. the records of every zou-esh's missions, the archived knowledge of the world below the rim accumulated across centuries, and the working documents of the order's protocols and refinements all reside at the school. retired zou-esh in their post-active second careers staff the archive, train the next cohort, and refine the order's practices based on what they learned in their seventy-five missions of fieldwork.

the school's name is unusual in kahu-tok for being the only major institution named for an individual. the language's philosophical commitments otherwise work against personalization at this scale. the exception is intentional.
