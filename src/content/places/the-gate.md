the gate is the chokepoint where the long climb tops out onto the nu-tera plateau. it is the only ascent path from the world below the spore line to the kahu sanctuary above. the long climb is narrow throughout and impassable in winter. the gate is where the climb ends and the plateau begins.

the modern zou-esh live and train at the gate. this is structural. the zou-esh are the order that crosses thresholds, and the gate is the threshold of <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span> itself. defending the gate is the defining role of the order. mara-skol, the training institution of the zou-esh, is at the gate, not on the broader plateau.

mara died at the gate. her last mission ended at the chokepoint where the long climb meets the plateau, when raiders caught her zou-esh group returning from below. she commanded her team to continue without her with the cry <span class="kt">!kia-vyl</span>, "live," and fought the raiders alone. she killed them. she was mortally wounded. she did not move from where she stood. she died at the place she had defended.

the inscription on the stone at the gate, <span class="kt">!kia-vyl</span>, is mara's last command, scrawled into the rock at the place she gave it. it is not commemoration. every modern zou-esh trains at the place where the cats first entered kahu life, where mara died defending tora vyl drak, and where the inscription she left still names what they exist to do. the continuity is not symbolic. the order is practicing mara every day.

the gate also serves as the quarantine point for newcomers. the zou-esh bring survivors up the long climb from the world below, where prolonged exposure to <span class="kt">peli-shen</span> has affected almost everyone. at the gate, in the quarantine zone of nu-tera adjacent to the zou-esh compound, newcomers are held under observation while the neurotoxicity clears and the order assesses their integration. the criteria and duration of quarantine are open canon. nothing of <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span> is shared with a newcomer until detox is complete and the person is themselves again.
