the threshold. the eastern quadrant of the caldera floor, the transitional country where <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-stone-climb">the stone climb</a> tops the inner face. thinner-settled than the other quadrants, it is the caldera's front door and carries the traffic of everything that enters or leaves. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a>, the order of thresholds, consider the quadrant informally theirs, and the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> let them.
