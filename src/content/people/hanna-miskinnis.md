hanna miskinnis discovered biological immortality. her research group, working in the years before the fracture, developed the telomere intervention that allowed the human body to halt the cellular aging process. the discovery was published, briefly, before the technology was monopolized by the ultra-wealthy and disappeared behind the walls of the arcologies that would become the longs' permanent home.

hanna refused to use the intervention herself. the reason she gave, in the records that survive, was that what she had built was for the species and not for the individual. she would not be the first to take it. it is not recorded whether she expected to be one of the last who could have refused.

after the fracture, after the leveling, after the longs were sealed in and the world above forgot them, hanna's name traveled differently than her work did. the longs carried the intervention. the kahu carried the refusal. when the schism arose more than two thousand years later, it was hanna's refusal that the purists invoked as their founding precedent. she had named the line. they would hold it.

the kahu's own longevity tradition descends from a separate and later development, the viral and bacterial agents that suppress disease across long life. that path is not telomere intervention and is not hanna's. but the kahu who hold the limit, who refuse the further step, place themselves in hanna's lineage by inheritance of conviction rather than of method.
