the longs are the pre-collapse ultra-wealthy who monopolized the miskinnis longevity intervention in the years before <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a>. they are the immortal elite who took the technology that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/hanna-miskinnis">hanna miskinnis</a> had refused to use and sealed themselves inside arcologies. after <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-leveling">the leveling</a> of approximately 2080 ad, they were sealed in further by the emp counter-strike that the surface survivors organized against them. they have been there ever since.

the longs were several thousand strong at the moment of the fracture. across the centuries since, attrition by accident, by isolation-induced collapse, and by the failure modes of small isolated populations across very long time spans has reduced them. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a>'s best estimate, derived from records the order has accumulated over centuries and from the rare salvage that has surfaced from older bunker locations, is that approximately one thousand longs are still alive in the present. the figure is rough.

the longs survive in militarized, originally-emp-hardened arcologies. continuity-of-government installations like cheyenne mountain, mount weather, raven rock, and their international equivalents in the swiss alps and the canadian shield. some of these installations were originally built for governments and were taken over by the longs as the pre-collapse state collapsed; others were the longs' private constructions from the start. the precise inventory of which arcologies survived in operational condition through the leveling and across the centuries since is open canon. some have certainly failed by now. some are likely still running.

the longs retain advanced pre-collapse technology that the rest of the world lost, including the miskinnis intervention itself and presumably whatever has been developed inside the arcologies across two and a half millennia of isolated technical work. they also lost what the rest of the world kept: open-air life, contact with people outside their fixed population, and the entry of new people into the community over time. what life inside a sealed environment with the same fixed population across two thousand five hundred years actually does to a human community is open canon.

the kahu have no working knowledge of the longs as a living people. what the kahu know is the pre-collapse history: that in the years of <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-great-devaluation">the great devaluation</a>, the ultra-wealthy who monopolized the miskinnis longevity intervention sealed themselves into hardened arcologies, and that the surface answered with <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-leveling">the leveling</a> that sealed them in for good. this much is preserved through the records that came down with the bunker survivors. but the kahu read it as history, a thing that happened and ended, the way they read the devaluation itself. they do not know the longs are still alive behind those walls. no zou-esh expedition has reached an operational arcology; the installations are far from <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span> or sealed past any entry the order has found, and nothing has come out of them to tell the kahu otherwise. the longs, to the kahu, are a closed chapter. that the chapter is not closed is something only the reader knows.

the kahu's purist tradition descends from hanna miskinnis's refusal, which preceded the monopolization of her work. the purists invoke hanna; the longs they invoke are the historical longs, the pre-fracture example of what happens when a small group claims for itself an immortality the rest of humanity does not get. they do not imagine living longs behind surviving walls, because they do not know there are any. the technologists do not engage with the longs at all. the longs are not <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-schism">the schism</a>'s present reference point. they are the historical condition that made the schism's underlying questions sharp enough to matter.

the two peoples reached long life by different roads, and this matters to the kahu even though they do not know the longs survive to embody the contrast. the longs hold the miskinnis intervention, hanna's telomere work, the halting of cellular aging, pre-fracture machinery carried unchanged behind sealed doors. the kahu's longevity is a separate and later thing, the viral and bacterial agents of <span class="kt">vyl-tapi</span> that suppress disease across a long life. one road is the old world's machine, kept; the other is the kahu's own living biotech, grown. the kahu walk the second road without ever having seen where the first one led.

whether the longs are aware that the kahu exist is open canon. the kahu's own unawareness of the longs is not open: they do not know.
