the longs are the pre-collapse ultra-wealthy who monopolized the miskinnis longevity intervention in the years before <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a>. they are the immortal elite who took the technology that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/hanna-miskinnis">hanna miskinnis</a> had refused to use and sealed themselves inside arcologies. after <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-leveling">the leveling</a> of approximately 2080 ad, they were sealed in further by the emp counter-strike that the surface survivors organized against them. they have been there ever since.

the longs were several thousand strong at the moment of the fracture. across the centuries since, attrition by accident, by isolation-induced collapse, and by the failure modes of small isolated populations across very long time spans has reduced them. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a>'s best estimate, derived from records the order has accumulated over centuries and from the rare salvage that has surfaced from older bunker locations, is that approximately one thousand longs are still alive in the present. the figure is rough.

the longs survive in militarized, originally-emp-hardened arcologies. continuity-of-government installations like cheyenne mountain, mount weather, raven rock, and their international equivalents in the swiss alps and the canadian shield. some of these installations were originally built for governments and were taken over by the longs as the pre-collapse state collapsed; others were the longs' private constructions from the start. the precise inventory of which arcologies survived in operational condition through the leveling and across the centuries since is open canon. some have certainly failed by now. some are likely still running.

the longs retain advanced pre-collapse technology that the rest of the world lost, including the miskinnis intervention itself and presumably whatever has been developed inside the arcologies across two and a half millennia of isolated technical work. they also lost what the rest of the world kept: open-air life, contact with people outside their fixed population, and the entry of new people into the community over time. what life inside a sealed environment with the same fixed population across two thousand five hundred years actually does to a human community is open canon.

the kahu have no working knowledge of the longs. the kahu's pre-collapse history is preserved through the records that came down with the bunker survivors and through whatever salvage the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> have recovered from below across the centuries. the longs' arcologies are not in zou-esh territory. they are far from <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span> in many cases, and where they are nearer, they are sealed enough that no zou-esh expedition has found a way in. the kahu know about the longs as a historical category in the same way they know about <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-great-devaluation">the great devaluation</a>: a feature of the pre-collapse world that helped shape what came after.

the kahu's purist tradition descends from hanna miskinnis's refusal, which preceded the longs' decision to use her work. the purists invoke hanna; they do not invoke the longs, except as the negative example of what happens when a small group claims an immortality the rest of humanity does not get. the technologists do not engage with the longs at all. the longs are not <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-schism">the schism</a>'s reference point. they are the historical condition that made the schism's underlying questions sharp enough to matter.

whether the longs are aware that the kahu exist is open canon.
