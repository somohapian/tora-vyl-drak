the cat-farmers are the small farming community living below the caldera who, when mara's zou-esh expedition found them during the age of bloom, were in possession of the serval-cougar hybrid cats that would become the kahu cats. their arrival in <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span> through mara's diplomatic mission is the founding moment of the partnership between the kahu and the cats.

the cat-farmers were a small group. precise size is open canon; the population was numerically modest by kahu standards, somewhere on the order of a few dozen to a few hundred. they had survived in the lowlands by maintaining a viable farming operation despite the conditions of the post-fracture world. their relationship to the cats was already partnership, not domestication; the cats had bonded to them across the generations of mutual survival, and the cat lineage had developed under their care into something distinctly different from the wild serval-cougar hybrid origins.

mara, leading a zou-esh expedition into the field, encountered the cat-farmers and assessed them. the standard zou-esh watch-first protocol applied. across days of observation, mara judged the cat-farmers worthy. she initiated contact. she offered the path up to <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span>. the cat-farmers accepted. mara brought them, with their cats, up the long climb to the gate.

at the gate they were quarantined according to the standard kahu protocol. the cats were quarantined alongside them. across the weeks that followed, the cat-farmers detoxed from prolonged <span class="kt">peli-shen</span> exposure and the kahu assessed them for integration. they were not fast-tracked. mara's mission did not give them special status above the standard pathway. they were settled into nu-tera, into the agricultural belt, at the standard pace.

the cats, however, spread upward and outward through other carriers over generations. in many cases the cat lineage moved up to the caldera before the human lineage of the cat-farmers did. the human community proceeded at the standard kahu integration pace. the cats integrated faster because the kahu wanted them.

the cat-kahu order, the small lineage-based community on the nu-tera wrap-around who hold the breeding, training, and stewardship of the kahu cats, traces its institutional continuity to the cat-farmers' arrival. whether the cat-kahu are direct lineal descendants of the cat-farmers, or whether the order absorbed other lineages over the centuries while preserving the cat-farmers' working knowledge, is open canon.
