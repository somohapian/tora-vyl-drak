akika was the partner of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bixel">bixel</a>. she was of a chinese family that had immigrated to russia. she was killed by the russian state around 2073, before <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a>, and never reached the caldera. her surname is not preserved; the record that would have carried it was destroyed with her. the deep-grief vocabulary of <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/kahu-tok">kahu-tok</a>, centered on the word <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/language/toska">toska</a></span>, grief, derives from bixel's mourning for her and was carried into the language bixel engineered.
