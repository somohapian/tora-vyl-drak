edi was the head of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> during the middle of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/age-of-bloom">age of bloom</a> and the figure who ended the kahu's first government. he took control of the caldera and the plateau around 3250, roughly eleven hundred years after the founding, without a battle. edi was an administrator rather than a soldier. his instrument was the order's dual mandate: as head of the zou-esh he held the council's only account of the world beyond the rim, and he held the gate. the council depended on his order for its entire picture of the outside. he never opened the gate.

edi converted the long deadlock between the caldera floor and the plateau into a series of temporary coordinating authorities, granted to him to make the paralyzed council function. each authority, once granted, was retained. the standing force of the order sat behind him throughout. by the time the council recognized what had happened, it had signed its own powers into his offices, on the record and within the law, and the republic was a formality only.

the capture was almost bloodless, and most kahu did not register it. food supply, the gate, the strains, and the naming continued unchanged. the capture was procedural, and it was the political class, not the population, that felt the change. resistance was small. among those who opposed edi, while still a zou-esh trainee, was <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mara">mara</a>.

the resistance prevailed. edi was deposed and sent into exile, provisioned to survive, in keeping with the kahu refusal of capital punishment. out of the settlement that followed came the kahu's second government, the bicameral <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-council">council</a> of the present, built so that no head of any order could take the sanctuary again (see <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-seizure">the seizure</a>).

the kahu retire a name in honor, as they did for mara and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bixel">bixel</a>. edi was denied this. his name was never retired and remains in the common pool, free to be given to any child.
