yelena cruz was a linguist of the early <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/age-of-tapestry">age of tapestry</a> and a student of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bixel">bixel</a>. of mixed founding descent, she was born around 2122 ad. after bixel's death she completed and formalized <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/kahu-tok">kahu-tok</a>, carrying bixel's engineered core, the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-bixel-1000">bixel 1000</a>, to a stable and teachable standard and overseeing the period in which the language displaced the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-bastard-tongue">bastard tongue</a>. her work ran from roughly the 2160s to the 2190s. she died around 2195. cruz was known in the founding record and in her own time, but she does not appear in the kahu's later account of how kahu-tok came to be, which credits bixel alone.
