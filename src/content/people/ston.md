ston is named in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> record. the records currently available do not yet fix the role, the dates, or the era. this entry is a placeholder for the figure and will be expanded as records are recovered.

the word <span class="kt">ston</span> in kahu-tok means rock or stone, and is the root of compound terms such as <span class="kt">ston-ropa</span> (rock-clothes, the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> shear-thickening armor cloth) and <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/ston-shen">ston-shen</a></span> (the chemosynthetic lichen-bacteria paste that sustained the bunker survivors of <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/the-dark-century">the dark century</a>). whether the name of the person ston traces back to the same root, or is a separate inheritance, is open canon.
