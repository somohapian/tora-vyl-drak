the schism is the unfolding theological and medical disagreement within the kahu about how much biological intervention is appropriate. it is the central live conflict of the present moment in <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span>.

the disagreement is not about whether kahu biotech is permitted. all kahu use the viral and bacterial agents that the warden-tenders maintain. these agents handle hygiene, food optimization, disease suppression, and the baseline of daily medical care. without them, the kahu population could not survive at its current scale and density. nobody disputes this.

the disagreement is about how far the intervention should go. specifically, it is about lifespan extension and aggressive medical intervention in the second half of a long kahu life.

the **technologists** embrace the full agent stack. they argue that the same logic that justifies suppressing disease in a child justifies suppressing the cumulative damage of age in an elder. the agents are kahu work. they are made by the community for the community. extending good years is what the community is for. lifespans into the two hundreds are the technologist horizon.

the **purists** draw a line. they argue that there is a proper duration to a human life, and that extending it past that duration is a betrayal not of biology but of meaning. the agents are still good. the line is past the agents, somewhere in the territory of aggressive intervention. where exactly the line falls is contested even among purists. the principle is that there is a line and the kahu must hold it.

the purists trace their founding precedent to hanna miskinnis, the pre-impact researcher whose telomere intervention became the basis for the longs' immortality. hanna refused to use her own intervention. the kahu purists invoke her refusal as the founding gesture of their position. her work is not the kahu work; the kahu's longevity is from disease suppression, not from telomere intervention. but her refusal is the lineage.

the schism is a religious disagreement, not a luddite one. it is conducted in the council and at every kahu hearth. both sides are kahu. both sides care for the community above their own preference. the disagreement is about what the community is for at its outer edge.

the caldera shudder of approximately 4525 ad destabilized the kahu's absolute sense of geological security and is generally credited with accelerating the schism into its current form. an earthquake under the sanctuary is a different kind of evidence about what duration anything has. both factions read the shudder as confirmation of their position.
