the schism is the unfolding theological and medical disagreement within the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> about how much biological intervention is appropriate. it is the central live conflict of the present moment in <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>.

the disagreement is not about whether kahu biotech is permitted. all kahu use the viral and bacterial agents that <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-warden-tenders">the warden-tenders</a> maintain. these agents handle hygiene, food optimization, disease suppression, and the baseline of daily medical care. without them, the kahu population could not survive at its current scale and density. nobody disputes this.

the disagreement is about how far the intervention should go. specifically, it is about lifespan extension and aggressive medical intervention in the second half of a long kahu life.

the agent stack has lengthened kahu lives slowly for as long as records run. a kahu of the present endura can expect two hundred years; the average age at death is about two hundred eight; the oldest kahu in the record died at two hundred thirty-seven. the technologists propose the next generation of the stack, reaching deliberately past two hundred fifty. the purists answer that there is a direction in which longer stops being living, and that the kahu have a name they refuse to say for the people who went that way. neither faction disputes the numbers. the dispute is what the numbers are for.<sup class="cite"><a href="#src-1" id="ref-1">1</a></sup>

the **technologists** support the full agent stack. they argue that the same logic that justifies suppressing disease in a child justifies suppressing the cumulative damage of age in an elder. they hold that the agents are kahu work, made by the community for the community, and that extending years of good health is a proper use of them.

the **purists** hold that intervention should be limited. they argue that there is a proper duration to a human life, and that extending it past that duration is a betrayal of meaning rather than of biology. they accept the agents. they place the limit past the agents, in the territory of aggressive intervention. where exactly the limit falls is contested even among purists. their principle is that a limit exists and the kahu must observe it.

the purists trace their founding precedent to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/hanna-miskinnis">hanna miskinnis</a>, the pre-impact researcher whose telomere intervention became the basis for <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/the-longs">the longs</a>' immortality. hanna refused to use her own intervention. the kahu purists cite her refusal as the founding precedent of their position. her work is not the kahu work; kahu longevity derives from disease suppression, not from telomere intervention. her refusal is the precedent they invoke.<sup class="cite"><a href="#src-2" id="ref-2">2</a></sup>

the schism is a religious disagreement, not an opposition to technology as such. it is conducted in <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-council">the council</a> and at kahu hearths. both sides are kahu. both sides prioritize the community over their own preference. the disagreement concerns the appropriate limit of intervention.

<a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-caldera-shudder">the caldera shudder</a> of approximately 4525 ad destabilized the kahu's sense of geological security and is generally credited with accelerating the schism into its current form. both factions read the shudder as confirmation of their position.<sup class="cite"><a href="#src-3" id="ref-3">3</a></sup>
