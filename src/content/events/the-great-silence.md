the great silence is the kahu term for the global electromagnetic pulse that ended the age of electricity in the moments of the fracture. the silence happened in the same minutes as the impacts, on november 10, 2075. it is not a separate event from the fracture; it is one of the fracture's three consequences, alongside the immediate impact destruction and the long-term <span class="kt">peli-shen</span> emergence. but it is recorded as a separate event because what it did was distinct, what it ended was distinct, and how the kahu carry it forward is distinct.

both fragments of the cleaved comet punched through earth's atmosphere at hypersonic speeds. friction superheated the air into a highly energetic plasma sheath around each. when the plasma channels collapsed on impact, they interacted violently with the earth's magnetic field. the result was overlapping planet-wide electromagnetic pulses.

the silence fried the global electrical grid. it fried the communication satellites. it fried the internet's physical infrastructure. it fried unshielded modern electronics worldwide: vehicles, appliances, medical devices, weapons systems, the embedded computers in industrial machinery. emp-hardened systems in deep military bunkers, in some arcologies of the longs, and in occasional foresighted civilian preparations survived. the surface, broadly, did not.

the immediate consequence was that survivor pockets became isolated. coordinated response to the fracture's other consequences became impossible. the population had no way of knowing what had happened beyond the horizon of what they could see and hear directly. communication regressed by approximately two centuries in a single afternoon.

the great silence is what gives the dark century its name. the long-term consequence is what made the world the kahu would later inherit: a world without electricity, without long-range communication, without the technological substrate of the pre-collapse civilization. the kahu's own technological tradition, biotech and growth rather than electronics and broadcast, is in part a response to having no choice in the matter. the silence took the alternative path off the table.

the silence does not end. no one has restored the grid. the post-collapse world is the post-silence world.
