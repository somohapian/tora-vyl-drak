the great silence is the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> term for the global electromagnetic pulse that ended the age of electricity in the moments of <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a>. the silence occurred in the same minutes as the impacts, on november 10, 2075. it is not a separate event from the fracture; it is one of the fracture's three consequences, alongside the immediate impact destruction and the long-term <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span> emergence. it is recorded as a separate event because its effects, what it ended, and how the kahu carry it forward are distinct.

both fragments of the cleaved comet punched through earth's atmosphere at hypersonic speeds. friction superheated the air into a highly energetic plasma sheath around each. when the plasma channels collapsed on impact, they interacted violently with the earth's magnetic field. the result was overlapping planet-wide electromagnetic pulses.

the silence disabled the global electrical grid, the communication satellites, and the internet's physical infrastructure. it disabled unshielded modern electronics worldwide: vehicles, appliances, medical devices, weapons systems, and the embedded computers in industrial machinery. emp-hardened systems in deep military bunkers, in some arcologies of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/the-longs">the longs</a>, and in some foresighted civilian preparations survived. most surface electronics did not.

the immediate consequence was that survivor pockets became isolated. coordinated response to the fracture's other consequences became impossible. the population had no way of knowing what had happened beyond what they could observe directly. communication capacity regressed by approximately two centuries within a single day.

the great silence is the source of the name of <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/the-dark-century">the dark century</a>. its long-term consequence shaped the world the kahu would later inherit: a world without electricity, without long-range communication, and without the technological substrate of the pre-collapse civilization. the kahu technological tradition, based on biotech and growth rather than electronics and broadcast, is in part a result of these conditions.

the silence has not ended. no one has restored the grid. the post-collapse world is the post-silence world.
