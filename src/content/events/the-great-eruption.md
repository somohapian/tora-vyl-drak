the great eruption is the geological event that formed <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>. it occurred in 2075, roughly five weeks after <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a> and the cascadia impact of november 10.

the cascadia fragment of the fracture impacted the juan de fuca plate off the oregon coast on november 10, 2075. the impact triggered a magnitude nine or greater megathrust earthquake and destabilized the subterranean magma chambers of <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-cascade">the cascade</a> volcanic arc. weeks of escalating subduction-zone earthquakes followed as the chambers moved toward release. the arc gave way roughly five weeks and two days after the impact.

the eruption that followed was vei-8, a super-eruption of the largest scale known in earth's geological record. it vaporized thousands of square miles of toxic surface soil contaminated by both the heavy-metal fallout of the fracture and the early spread of <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span>. the eruption blew an enormous dust column into the upper atmosphere and generated a regional ash deposit. the megathrust released with the eruption raised the high mountain walls along the western side, drove the pacific inland to form the great western sea, and uplifted the plateau. the magma chamber then collapsed inward as it emptied, forming the eighteen-mile sterile caldera the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> would later call <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span>.

the bunker survivors of the idaho rockies and the blue mountains of eastern oregon observed the eruption from the surrounding peaks. they were close enough to be affected by ash fall, atmospheric disturbance, and ecological consequences in their own elevation band. their immediate response was self-protective. the long-term consequence, that the eruption had cleared an area they would eventually be able to inhabit safely, became apparent across the decades of observation that followed. the long watch was the period during which they maintained that observation.

the eruption sterilized the caldera floor of every prior contamination. when the kahu ancestors descended, around 2120 ad, they entered a basin that had been sterilized by the same event that formed it.
