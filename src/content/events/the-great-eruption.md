the great eruption is the geological event that formed <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span>. it occurred in approximately 2133 ad, fifty-eight years after the fracture and the cascadia impact of 2075.

the cascadia fragment of the fracture impacted the juan de fuca plate off the oregon coast in november of 2075. that impact triggered a magnitude nine or greater megathrust earthquake and critically destabilized the subterranean magma chambers of the cascade volcanic arc. the destabilization did not express itself immediately. magma chambers under load take time to find the path of least resistance to release. fifty-eight years was the time it took.

the eruption that followed was vei-8, a super-eruption of the largest scale known in earth's geological record. it vaporized thousands of square miles of toxic surface soil contaminated by both the heavy-metal fallout of the fracture and the early spread of <span class="kt">peli-shen</span>. the eruption blew an enormous dust column into the upper atmosphere, generated a regional ash deposit, and ultimately collapsed inward as the magma chamber emptied. the inward collapse formed the eighteen-mile sterile caldera the kahu would later call <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span>.

the bunker survivors of the idaho rockies and the blue mountains of eastern oregon watched the eruption from the surrounding peaks. they were close enough to know it had happened. they were also close enough to be affected by ash fall, by atmospheric disturbance, by the immediate ecological consequences in their own elevation band. their immediate reaction was self-protective; the long-term consequence, that the eruption had cleared a place they would eventually be able to live in safely, was visible only across the next century and a half of observation. the long watch was the period during which they kept that observation up.

the eruption sterilized the caldera floor of every prior contamination. when the kahu ancestors descended in 2283 ad, they were entering a basin that had been wiped clean by the same event that had created it.
