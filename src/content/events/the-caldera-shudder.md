the caldera shudder is the violent subterranean seismic warning that struck beneath <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span> in approximately 4525 ad, one hundred years before the present.

before the shudder, the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> had lived inside the caldera for nearly twenty-five hundred years without the underlying volcano producing detectable seismic activity. the mountain was mostly dormant. its residual heat ran the <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/hot-kasa">hot-kasa</a> thermal grid. the kahu had built their two hundred and forty thousand-strong settlement into the rock of the rim on the assumption that the geological quiet would continue.

the shudder ended that assumption. the event was severe enough to damage infrastructure, contaminate groundwater in some sectors, and temporarily reduce productivity on the <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/sun-tera">sun-tera</a>. the exact magnitude and duration are open canon.

the kahu repaired the damage. the geothermal grid stabilized. the agricultural output recovered. nothing further has happened in the hundred years since. the kahu food economy, even with caloric surplus, runs on conservative reserves and careful rotation because the event is in living memory. the people who lived through the shudder are still alive under kahu biotech.

the shudder accelerated <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-schism">the schism</a>, the unfolding kahu disagreement about biological intervention and the proper duration of life, which took its modern shape in the decades after the shudder. both factions read the shudder as confirmation of their position. the technologists took it as reason to build resilience into the body, since the sanctuary itself was not the guarantee the kahu had thought. the purists took it as reason to hold to a defined life, since indefinite duration in a place that could yet erupt was a form of denial.

whether the cause of the shudder was movement in the residual magma chamber beneath <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/hart-ston">hart-ston</a>, in the surrounding tectonics, or both, is open canon. <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-warden-tenders">the warden-tenders</a> observe hart-ston's signs and the geothermal grid continuously. <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-council">the council</a> reviews their reports. nothing further has been reported.
