the caldera shudder is the violent subterranean seismic warning that struck beneath <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span> in approximately 4525 ad, one hundred years before the present.

before the shudder, the kahu had lived inside the caldera for nearly twenty-five hundred years without the underlying volcano announcing itself. the mountain was mostly dead but slightly alive. its residual heat ran the hot-kasa thermal grid; its quiet was the conditions of kahu life. the kahu had built their two hundred and forty thousand-strong settlement into the rock of the rim on the working assumption that the quiet would continue.

the shudder broke the assumption. the event itself was severe enough to damage infrastructure, contaminate groundwater in some sectors, and temporarily reduce productivity on the sun-tera. the exact magnitude and duration are open canon. what is not open is the meaning. the caldera could speak. it had spoken.

the kahu repaired the damage. the geothermal grid stabilized. the agricultural output recovered. nothing further has happened in the hundred years since. but the kahu food economy, even with caloric surplus, runs on conservative reserves and careful rotation because catastrophe is in living memory. the people who lived through the shudder are still alive under kahu biotech.

the deeper consequence of the shudder is what it accelerated. the schism, the unfolding kahu disagreement about biological intervention and the proper duration of life, took its modern shape in the decades after the shudder. both factions read the shudder as confirmation of their position. the technologists saw further reason to build resilience into the body, since the sanctuary itself was not the guarantee the kahu had thought. the purists saw further reason to hold to a defined life, since infinite duration in a place that could yet erupt was a particular kind of denial.

whether the cause of the shudder was movement in the residual magma chamber beneath hart-ston, in the surrounding tectonics, or both, is open canon. the warden-tenders observe hart-ston's signs and the geothermal grid continuously. the council reviews their reports. nothing further has been reported.
