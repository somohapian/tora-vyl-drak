the fracture is the event that ended the pre-collapse world. on november 10, 2075, at approximately 07:33 utc, the combined nuclear arsenals of the united states, russia, and china executed a coordinated launch. they had detected a seven-kilometer dead comet nucleus from the oort cloud on a sungrazer trajectory toward earth, undetected until weeks before its arrival because it approached from the glare of the sun and presented a dark non-reflective surface. spectroscopy in the final days established that the object was rich in iron, nickel, iridium, platinum, and radioactive heavy metals. impact would have spread globally fatal radioactive contamination.

the volley launched stripped icbms outfitted with maximum-yield thermonuclear devices, targeting a massive internal fault line within the object. at 08:18 utc the weapons detonated. the volley did not vaporize the comet; the object was too massive. it cleaved it into two continent-killing fragments on slightly divergent paths. the outcome was two marginally survivable impacts rather than total sterilization.

at 08:37 utc, a three-kilometer fragment impacted the english channel off the coast of normandy. the atmospheric blast and the immediate tsunami obliterated paris, london, and the european lowlands within hours. structural collapses rippled across eurasia.

at 08:40 utc, a four-kilometer fragment impacted the juan de fuca plate off the oregon coast. the impact triggered a magnitude nine or greater megathrust earthquake, liquefied the pacific northwest's coastal cities, and destabilized the subterranean magma chambers of <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-cascade">the cascade</a> volcanic arc. weeks of escalating subduction-zone earthquakes followed. roughly five weeks after the impact, the arc released in a vei-8 super-eruption, recorded separately as <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-great-eruption">the great eruption</a>. the megathrust that came with the release raised the high western mountain walls, drove the pacific inland to form the great western sea, and collapsed the plateau and the caldera that would become <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>.

as both fragments passed through the atmosphere at hypersonic speeds, friction superheated the air into a highly energetic plasma sheath around each. on ground and ocean impact, the plasma channels collapsed and interacted with the earth's magnetic field. the result was overlapping planet-wide electromagnetic pulses that disabled the global electrical grid, communication satellites, internet infrastructure, and unshielded modern electronics. this is what the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> call <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-great-silence">the great silence</a>.

approximately half the world died in the impacts themselves. nine in ten of the survivors died over the following years from cold and starvation in <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-hyper-winter">the hyper-winter</a> that followed. <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/the-dark-century">the dark century</a> had begun.
