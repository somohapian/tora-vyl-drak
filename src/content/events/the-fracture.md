the fracture is the event that ended the world before. on november 10, 2075, at approximately 07:33 utc, the combined nuclear arsenals of the united states, russia, and china executed humanity's final act of global coordination. they had detected a seven-kilometer dead comet nucleus from the oort cloud on a sungrazer trajectory toward earth, invisible until weeks before its arrival because it approached from the glare of the sun and presented a dark non-reflective surface. spectroscopy in the final days established that the object was rich in iron, nickel, iridium, platinum, and radioactive heavy metals. impact would mean a globally fatal dirty bomb.

the volley launched stripped icbms outfitted with maximum-yield thermonuclear devices, targeting a massive internal fault line within the object. at 08:18 utc the weapons detonated. the volley did not vaporize the comet; the object was far too massive. it cleaved it into two continent-killing projectiles on slightly divergent paths. the choice was two marginally survivable apocalypses over total sterilization.

at 08:37 utc, a three-kilometer fragment impacted the english channel off the coast of normandy. the atmospheric blast and the immediate tsunami obliterated paris, london, and the european lowlands within hours. structural collapses rippled across eurasia.

at 08:40 utc, a four-kilometer fragment impacted the juan de fuca plate off the oregon coast. the impact triggered a magnitude nine or greater megathrust earthquake, liquefied the pacific northwest's coastal cities, and caused immediate coastal subsidence. the pacific roared inland and created what would become the great western sea, flooding the willamette valley. the same impact critically destabilized the subterranean magma chambers of <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-cascade">the cascade</a> volcanic arc. the destabilization would take fifty-eight years to express itself; <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-great-eruption">the great eruption</a> that formed <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span> would not occur until 2133 ad.

as both fragments punched through the atmosphere at hypersonic speeds, friction superheated the air into a highly energetic plasma sheath around each. on ground and ocean impact, the plasma channels abruptly collapsed and interacted with the earth's magnetic field. the result was overlapping planet-wide electromagnetic pulses that fried the global electrical grid, communication satellites, internet infrastructure, and unshielded modern electronics. the planet went dark. this is what the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> call <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-great-silence">the great silence</a>.

approximately half the world died in the impacts themselves. nine in ten of the survivors died over the following years from cold and starvation in <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-hyper-winter">the hyper-winter</a> that followed. <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/the-dark-century">the dark century</a> had begun.
