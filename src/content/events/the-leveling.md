the leveling is the event by which the surface survivors of <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a> removed from <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/the-longs">the longs</a> the capacity to rule the surface again.

in the years before the impact, the ultra-wealthy who had monopolized the miskinnis longevity intervention had retreated into hyper-secure, self-sustaining, emp-hardened arcologies. when the fracture occurred, the longs observed it through shielded sensors and chose total isolation. they retained advanced technology, indefinite life, and the capacity, in principle, to emerge later and re-subjugate whatever remained of the surface.

approximately five years after the impact, surface factions coordinated a targeted electromagnetic pulse counter-strike against the arcologies. the strike used emp-capable weapons that surface communities had retained, in some cases by salvage and in some cases by foresight; the precise inventory and provenance is open canon. the strike succeeded.

the leveling did not destroy the longs. their arcologies remained intact and their personal lives continued. the strike destroyed their external capability: their sensors, their communications, and their ability to project force beyond their walls were all disabled. the longs remained alive, wealthy, and immortal inside arcologies they could no longer monitor the outside from and could no longer usefully leave.

this is the act that sealed them in permanently. it is also the act by which the surface remnants, who would become the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> among others, secured the surface for themselves. the kahu inherit the world the leveling produced. the longs remain in their bunkers, outside it.

the kahu have no working memory of the leveling. by the time of <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-first-descent">the first descent</a>, around 2120 ad, more than four decades had passed and the longs had been forgotten. the kahu carry the leveling forward as the historical condition that allowed them to exist, not as a story they tell about themselves.
