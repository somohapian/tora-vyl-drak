name: the leveling
year: approximately 2080 ad
era: dark century
summary: the coordinated emp counter-strike by surface survivors against the longs' arcologies. the act that sealed the longs in permanently.
open_canon:
  - the identities of the surface coalitions that organized the strike

  - whether any longs' arcology survived intact and remains active
---

the leveling is the event by which the surface survivors of the fracture took back from the longs the ability to ever rule the surface again.

in the years before the impact, the ultra-wealthy who had monopolized the miskinnis longevity intervention had retreated into hyper-secure, self-sustaining, emp-hardened arcologies. when the fracture came, the longs watched the apocalypse through shielded sensors and chose total isolation. they retained advanced technology, indefinite life, and the ability, in principle, to emerge later and re-subjugate whatever remained of the surface.

approximately five years after the impact, surface factions coordinated a desperate, targeted electromagnetic pulse counter-strike against the arcologies. the strike used emp-capable weapons that surface communities had retained, in some cases by salvage and in some cases by foresight; the precise inventory and provenance is open canon. the strike succeeded.

the leveling did not destroy the longs. their arcologies remained intact and their personal lives continued. what the strike destroyed was their external capability. their sensors, their communications, their ability to project force beyond their walls, all stripped. the longs were left alive and rich and immortal inside boxes they could no longer see out of and could no longer leave usefully.

this is the act that sealed them in permanently. it is also the act by which the surface remnants, who would become the kahu among others, claimed the future for themselves. the kahu inherit the world the leveling made. the longs, in their bunkers, persist outside it.

the kahu themselves have no working memory of the leveling. by the time the first descent happened in 2283 ad, two hundred years had passed and the longs had been forgotten. the kahu carry the leveling forward as the historical condition that allowed them to exist. they do not carry it as a story they tell about themselves.
