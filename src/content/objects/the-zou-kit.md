the zou-kit is the standard field equipment of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a>. the kit is the outward sign of the order. it is also the technological foundation that allows the order to operate below the <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/peli-shen"><span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span></a> spore line and to defend <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-gate">the gate</a> against threats arriving from there. the kit is entirely living cloth and biotech. no microchips, no mechanical electronics. the bunker tradition of retro-bio-futurism in a single working outfit.

**the cloth mask.** filters <span class="kt">peli-shen</span> at concentrations encountered below five thousand feet. allows free movement in the toxic lowlands without effect. the kahu-tok name for the mask is open canon.

**klin-soma.** the bio-monitoring undersuit. tracks the zou-esh's body state during operations. the name reads, in kahu-tok, clean-body or clean-living-body, indicating the suit's continuous diagnostic function. the precise data the suit gathers and reports is open canon.

**ston-ropa.** the shear-thickening cloth that forms the zou-esh shirts and pants. the name reads rock-clothes. the cloth is thin and looks like ordinary clothing. under slow pressure it yields; a finger can push into it. under sudden impact it resists like rock. a slap, a strike, an arrow, all met with stiffness approaching solid material at the moment of contact. the effect is produced by an algae-derived component woven into or saturating the textile, part of the same biotech tradition that produces <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> medicine and food. the armor is invisible until invoked.

**mirar-sin-ropa.** the passive camouflage cloak. the name reads see-without-clothes or no-see-clothes, indicating the function. the mechanism by which the cloak achieves its effect is open canon. the practical result is that a zou-esh on stationary watch is difficult for an observer to distinguish from terrain.

**soma-ropa.** the layered suit as a whole, encompassing the undersuit, the armor cloth, and the cloak. the kahu-tok name reads body-clothes.

**the gloves.** the gloves adhere to the weapon and to the bow, allowing controlled retention through complex movement and clean transitions from the bow to its broken-down configuration without the zou-esh losing hold. zou-esh learn over years of training to use the adhesion strategically. the kahu-tok name for the gloves and the precise nature of the adhesion mechanism, biotech or mechanical, is open canon.

**jefe-tri.** the personalized bow that breaks down into a close-quarters weapon. the bow is four to seven feet long by personal preference. the trinity lock that opens it is unique to each zou-esh, trained on first, long before the actual weapon is designed; the weapon is born from the motion that opens it. the broken-down configuration is two blades, blade plus baton, or two batons, by personal preference. the configuration reflects the zou-esh's fighting style and temperament. the bow is designed and recrafted across training; by graduation it is the physical record of the zou-esh's own development. no one outside the order can separate the bow at all. another zou-esh might, with effort, by reading the lock's style. a fallen zou-esh's weapon is presumed unrecoverable except by the order itself.

**<a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/air-klin">air-klin</a>.** respiratory biome inoculation administered before deep or sustained operations below the spore line, purged from the body post-mission. parallels the pre-mission inoculation administered by <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-cat-kahu">the cat-kahu</a> to the kahu cat that accompanies the zou-esh into the lowlands. both treatments serve the same function: a temporary biological shield, applied before the mission, cleared after.
