<span class="kt">air-klin</span> is the respiratory biome inoculation administered to zou-esh before deep or sustained operations below the <span class="kt">peli-shen</span> spore line. the name in kahu-tok reads clean-air or air-clean. it is part of the zou-esh's layered defenses against the fungus that has rendered the lowlands lethal.

a zou-esh going below five thousand feet relies on three layers against <span class="kt">peli-shen</span>. the cloth mask filters spores at concentrations encountered in the lowlands. the kahu cat that accompanies the zou-esh handles the toxin through a hybrid system of native resistance bred over a thousand years and pre-mission inoculation by the cat-kahu. and the zou-esh's own body is protected, internally, by air-klin.

air-klin is a biological intervention rather than a barrier. before a deep-field mission, the zou-esh receives the inoculation. it establishes a temporary modification of the respiratory biome, the microorganisms that line the lungs and the upper airways. the modification establishes conditions hostile to <span class="kt">peli-shen</span>'s spore germination at the tissue interface. spores that the mask fails to filter, or that are inhaled during periods when the mask is removed, encounter a biome that does not allow them to establish. they are cleared rather than colonizing.

the protection is not permanent. air-klin's protective effect attenuates over the days and weeks of a mission. for short missions the inoculation lasts the entire deployment. for longer missions a zou-esh may carry a stable form of the inoculation to readminister in the field, though the exact protocol is open canon.

after the mission, when the zou-esh returns to the gate, the inoculation is cleared from the body as part of the standard post-mission care. the warden-tenders supply the antagonist that resolves the temporary biome modification and returns the zou-esh's lungs to their baseline state. carrying air-klin in the body is not a permanent condition; the kahu's general approach to biological interventions is that they should be applied for their purpose and then removed when their purpose is done.

air-klin's parallel is the cat-kahu's pre-mission inoculation of the kahu cat. same logical structure: temporary biological shield, applied before the mission, cleared after. the human and the cat both go into the lowlands prepared and both return to be unprepared again. nothing of the lowlands stays in either body longer than the mission requires.
