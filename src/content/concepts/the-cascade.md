the cascade is the volcanic arc that runs the length of what was, before <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a>, the pacific northwest of the united states and the canadian province of british columbia. the arc holds <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> call the range the bones of the cascade or simply the cascade.

the geographic significance of the cascade is total. the entire kahu sanctuary depends on the arc's specific combination of altitude and geothermal activity. above five thousand feet, the arc rises above the <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span> spore line. inside the calderas and the chamber networks of the older bunker locations, the magma chambers provide enough residual heat to make winter survivable. without the arc, the kahu do not exist.

the destabilization of the arc by the 2075 cascadia impact is what produced <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-great-eruption">the great eruption</a> of 2133 ad. the magnitude nine or greater megathrust earthquake of the impact day cracked the juan de fuca plate and reached into the subterranean magma chambers beneath the arc. fifty-eight years later, one of those chambers gave way in a vei-8 super-eruption. the kahu's sanctuary is the inward collapse of that eruption.

other peaks of the cascade are visible from the rim of <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span> on clear days. the kahu have kahu-tok names for the principal peaks; these names are open canon for the current entry. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> navigate the arc on their long missions and carry knowledge of the passes and the chamber networks that survive elsewhere.

the cascade also held the bunkers that produced the kahu ancestors. the deep mountain bunkers within the idaho rockies and the blue mountains of eastern oregon, where preppers, rural communities, and continuity-of-government personnel rode out <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-hyper-winter">the hyper-winter</a>, sat within the same geographic envelope that would eventually offer the caldera. the kahu's ancestors did not come down from the cascade. they came inward through it.
