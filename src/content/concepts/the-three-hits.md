the three hits are a tracking system internal to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a>. the order counts something in threes against each of its members, and the count carries consequence. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/fas-big">fas-big</a>, the order's unsanctioned rite of challenge, carries an automatic two hits, which places a single fas-big one short of the limit.

what exactly a hit measures is open canon. the system reads as a way the order holds its own accountable to the line between courage and recklessness, the same line <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mara">mara</a> walked and finally crossed. whether a hit marks accumulated field risk, a serious injury survived, a sanction for breaking the order's protocols, or some combination is not settled. what is established is the structure: three is the limit, and the fas-big spends two of them at once.

the system fits the order's character. the zou-esh hold a lifetime cap of seventy-five missions, set one short of three from mara's seventy-eight, a number that exists to keep a member from pushing past the point of return. the three hits read as the same instinct at a finer grain, a running count that keeps a zou-esh from spending themselves recklessly across a career rather than only at its end. the order that took the lesson of mara's death into its mission cap took it into its day-to-day accounting too.

what happens at the third hit, how hits are recorded, who keeps the count, and how the system relates to the mission cap are open canon.
