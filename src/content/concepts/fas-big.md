the fas-big is the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a>'s unsanctioned rite of challenge. the order does not authorize it, but the order recognizes it, and the order accounts for it: a zou-esh who undertakes the fas-big takes an automatic two hits in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-three-hits">three hits</a> system, which leaves them one short of the limit of three.

the rite sits in the gap between what the order forbids and what it sanctions. it is not part of the official path. it is something a zou-esh chooses to do outside the order's blessing, and the order's response is not to ban it but to price it. two hits is a heavy charge, heavy enough to make the fas-big a serious decision rather than a casual one, and the fact that the order charges it at all means the order has decided the rite will happen whether it approves or not, so it had better be counted.

this is a recognizably <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> way of handling a thing. the order does not pretend it can stop a member who is determined. it does not drive the rite underground by forbidding it outright. it acknowledges the rite, names it, and attaches a known cost, so that a zou-esh who chooses the fas-big does so with eyes open and the order's books stay honest.

the literal meaning of the roots <span class="kt">fas</span> and <span class="kt">big</span>, what the rite consists of, what a zou-esh seeks by undertaking it, and why it remains unsanctioned yet recognized are all open canon.
