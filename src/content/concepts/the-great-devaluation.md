the great devaluation is the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> term for the decades of institutional decay, information warfare, and environmental degradation that preceded <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a>.

the devaluation is understood by the kahu as a precondition for the events that followed. in the kahu account, the condition of a civilization at the moment of an impact shapes the survival, the recovery, and the long aftermath. the kahu's reading of the historical record is that humanity's response to the fracture, the volley that cleaved the comet, <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-leveling">the leveling</a> that sealed <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/the-longs">the longs</a> in, and the rise of the kahu themselves, was shaped by the condition of the civilization that 2075 found.

the devaluation spans, in approximate terms, the decades of the 2040s through the moment of the fracture in 2075. its features are recorded in the kahu archive in summary form. intense information warfare, ai-driven hyper-propaganda saturating public discourse, institutional trust collapses across governments and media and the professions, accelerating environmental degradation as ecological systems failed faster than political systems could respond. the population was numerically large and educated but unable to act coherently on the threats it could see coming.

the central technological event of the devaluation was the miskinnis discovery of biological immortality, traced to the work of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/hanna-miskinnis">hanna miskinnis</a> and her research group, completed in the period around 2051 to 2062 ad. the kahu identify this discovery as a point at which the trajectory of the devaluation might have changed. instead, the technology was monopolized by the ultra-wealthy. the longs took it behind walls. the rest of humanity received none of it, and the devaluation deepened. hanna refused to use her own work; the lineage of refusal that descends from her decision is the kahu purists' founding precedent.

the kahu archive treats the devaluation as a cautionary case, a civilization unable to save itself whose breakage was already partly underway before the comet completed it. the kahu's institutions are built, in part, against the failure modes of the devaluation. <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-council">the council</a>'s structure, <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-warden-tenders">the warden-tenders</a>' practice, and the language's refusal of certain conceptual moves are intended in part to avoid repeating the devaluation's mistakes.
