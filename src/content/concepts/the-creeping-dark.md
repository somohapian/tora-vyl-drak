the creeping dark is the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> name for the slow neurotoxic descent caused by accumulating <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span> exposure. it is what happens to the mind of a person who lives below five thousand feet.

<span class="kt">peli-shen</span> spores carry heavy-metal nanoparticles and unique mycotoxins. each breath at lowland concentration deposits a small dose into the body, and successive breaths add to it. the body cannot eliminate the load at the rate the lowlands impose. over weeks, months, and years, accumulation reaches the brain.

the early symptoms are pronounced but are easily mistaken. meth-like stimulation, increased agitation, paranoia, and mood swings. minor hallucinations that the affected person can often identify as hallucinations. metabolism and cognition are elevated. at this stage, the affected person remains recognizably themselves, though the change is becoming apparent to others.

the middle stages compound. the agitation deepens. the hallucinations become harder to dismiss. judgment degrades. memory loss begins, intermittently at first and then in larger blocks. the affected person begins to lose continuity with their own past, sometimes without being aware of the loss. the kahu phrase for this stage, translated into english, is that the person grows slower, then strange.

in the late stages the person becomes increasingly unwell, less able to function, less able to recognize the people they know, and less able to maintain the basic life skills that kept them alive in the lowlands. the result is a dementia indistinguishable from the late-stage dementias of pre-collapse medicine. the body's other systems then fail under the heavy-metal load, and the person dies.

the descent is reversible only in the early stages and only by moving above the spore line. the kahu's quarantine protocol at <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-gate">the gate</a> exists for this purpose. a newcomer brought up <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-long-climb">the long climb</a> is held in observation, supported by kahu biotech, while the accumulated load clears. the clearing process is slow, and the kahu allow the time it requires. nothing of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span> is shared with a newcomer until they have recovered.

newcomers who arrive too late, whose descent has gone too deep, do not recover. the kahu do not abandon them, but the question of what to do with them is among the order's most difficult. the precise practice varies and is not codified.

the creeping dark is the reason the kahu do not readily trust a person they have just brought up from the lowlands, and the reason they extend quarantine and biotech support to such people regardless.
