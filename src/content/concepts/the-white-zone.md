the white zone is the elevation above roughly five thousand feet that is effectively free of <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span>. it is the clean air, the safe ground, the band the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> live inside. its name marks the absence of the fungus: above the line, the spore does not hold.

below the white zone lies the fade band, the transitional stretch at roughly four thousand seven hundred and fifty to five thousand feet where peli-shen struggles to complete its life cycle but has not yet failed entirely. the fade band is the edge of the fungus's range, where the cold, the ultraviolet light, the dry alpine air, and the competition from native lichens together wear the fungus down without quite finishing it. it is neither the poisoned lowland nor the clean upland. it is the seam between them.

below the fade band is the lowland proper, where peli-shen thrives, where the heavy metals and radionuclides of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">fracture</a> pool in the valleys, and where anyone who lingers is drawn into <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-creeping-dark">the creeping dark</a>.

the three bands are the vertical structure of the post-fracture world. the white zone is where humanity survives. the fade band is the contested edge. the lowland is what the world became. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-goldilocks-filter">goldilocks filter</a> names the same boundary from the human side, the band that is survivable; the white zone and the fade band name it from the fungus's side, the elevations at which peli-shen fails and falters. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> who descend below the line into peli-shen's country wear the cloth mask precisely because they are leaving the white zone for the country of the spore.

the kahu-tok terms for the two bands, their exact elevations, and how they shift with the weather are open canon. so is whether the zou-esh ever work the fade band deliberately as a margin where the fungus is weak.
