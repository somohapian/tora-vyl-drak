the rebirth naming is a purist practice surrounding the release of names at death.

when a member of a purist <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> dies, the kahu watches for a child born close to the death, and close runs long for a long-lived people, up to a couple of years. that child receives the freed name, and the kahu feels the dead has been reborn in her, or at least that she carries part of the one who left.

the practice sits beside the mainstream custom of <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-naming">the naming</a> rather than inside it. in an ordinary naming the kahu meets the child first and finds the name that answers who arrived. in a rebirth naming the name answers the dead. most kahu regard this as a matter for each hearth to settle its own way, neither strange nor obligatory, simply the purist path.

the purists who keep this practice are the same faction connected to the prophecy. the prophecy's text, and how it bears on the rebirth naming, are open canon.
