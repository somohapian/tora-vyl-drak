the last walk is the gravest penalty in <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> justice. it is exile. the condemned is escorted by <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> beyond the rim of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span>, given tools, seeds, and knowledge, and left in the world below to live or die on their own. it is imposed by <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-pathfinders">the pathfinders</a>, the kahu's judicial council, and it is the end of the line in a system that is otherwise restorative and protective.

the kahu do not impose death. this is a deliberate moral choice, and the last walk is its expression. a people who hold all of their justice toward repair and protection still need a final answer for the person who cannot be repaired and from whom the community must be protected. that answer is removal, not killing. the condemned walks out with the means to survive. whether they do survive is not the kahu's to decide.

in the logic of the kahu's own language, the last walk is heavier than it looks to an outside reader. <span class="kt">kahu</span> means community at every scale, and the kahu worldview treats existence as existence-within-the-kahu. to be sent outside the kahu is, in the terms the language allows, to cease to be a person in the full sense. the last walk does not end a life. it ends a belonging, which the kahu consider the deeper thing. the exile keeps breathing and stops being kahu.

the escort by zou-esh is fitting. the zou-esh are the order that crosses the threshold, the ones who bring the worthy in through <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-gate">the gate</a>. for the last walk they run the motion in reverse, taking a person out through the same threshold they spend their lives defending. the order that decides who may enter is the order that carries out the rare decision that someone must leave.

the kahu-tok name for the last walk, the exact provision given to an exile, whether anyone has ever returned, and the circumstances that warrant it are open canon.
