the goldilocks filter is the band of elevation that the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> survive inside. it is high enough that <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/peli-shen">peli-shen</a></span> cannot complete its life cycle, and low enough that human life remains viable. the kahu live in the narrow window between the spore and the cold.

below the filter, the world belongs to peli-shen. the fungus thrives in the warm, polluted, low-altitude air where the heavy metals and radionuclides of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">fracture</a> accumulate, and anyone who lives there long is poisoned by degrees into <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-creeping-dark">the creeping dark</a>. above the filter, the air thins toward elevations where human settlement becomes its own struggle. between the two lies the band the kahu call home.

the lower edge of the band is the peli-shen kill line, at approximately five thousand feet, where temperature, ultraviolet exposure, and spore density together defeat the fungus. the caldera floor of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span> sits at six thousand feet, a thousand feet into the safe band. <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/nu-tera">nu-tera</a>, the plateau, sits just above five thousand, on the safe side of the line by a margin that is real but not generous, and that narrows or widens with the season and the year.

the filter is the precondition of the whole kahu world. the sanctuary is not chosen for beauty or for resources. it is chosen because it sits in the one band of elevation where people can live and the fungus cannot. everything else about the kahu, the agriculture, the settlement carved into the rim, the order that crosses the threshold to the poisoned world below, follows from the accident of an altitude that filters out the thing that kills.

the kahu-tok name for the filter, the upper bound of the viable band, and how the band shifts with the climate are open canon.
