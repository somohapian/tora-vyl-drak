warden is the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a>'s central decision-support system. it is a continent-scale biological supercomputer, realized during the <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/age-of-bloom">age of bloom</a>, that models the kahu's agriculture, infrastructure, ecology, demographics, and the long arc of consequences for the choices the kahu make.

warden is biological. its substrate is living, grown rather than built, maintained rather than programmed. the precise architecture is open canon. the practical experience of warden is that it can be consulted. <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-council">the council</a> consults warden on questions of food allocation, infrastructure investment, the management of <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-wilds">the wilds</a>, the response to environmental signals such as <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-caldera-shudder">the caldera shudder</a>. individual kahu consult warden on agricultural choices, breeding decisions for livestock and the cats, and the patterns of the seasons.

warden does not rule. the kahu rule themselves through the council and through the kahu structure at every scale. warden is the modeling layer, not the deciding layer. its recommendations are inputs to kahu deliberation, not substitutes for it. a council decision contrary to warden's recommendation is rare but not forbidden.

<a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-warden-tenders">the warden-tenders</a> are the kahu who care for warden's living systems. their work is upstream of every kahu decision that warden informs. without their continuous maintenance of the agents, the substrate, and the interfaces, warden's modeling capacity would degrade. the warden-tenders are an organization in their own right and are detailed in their own entry.

warden's name appears related to the english word for guardian. whether the name was chosen by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bixel">bixel</a> as part of the kahu-tok lexicon or was carried forward from the bastard tongue of <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/the-dark-century">the dark century</a> is open canon. the kahu treat the name as native.
