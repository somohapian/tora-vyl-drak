<a class="wiki-link" href="/language/peli-shen"><span class="kt">peli-shen</span></a> is the fungus that has rendered the lowlands lethal. the name in kahu-tok reads danger-life. it is the reason the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> live above five thousand feet.

the organism evolved in the years after <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a> from a native terrestrial fungus, most likely a lineage of aspergillus or penicillium. it was catalyzed by the massive influx of heavy metals from the shattered comet and fueled by hard radiation from the nuclear detonations. it adapted in the manner of the real-world radiotrophic fungi observed at chernobyl, learning to derive metabolic energy from ionizing radiation. it spread across a world emptied of biological competition.

the spores of <span class="kt">peli-shen</span> carry two toxicity vectors.

the first is **pneumo-toxicity**. severe chronic respiratory inflammation and scarring, leading eventually to a fatal lung disorder. this is the visible end-state of peli-shen exposure and the cause of most fatalities at the spore line.

the second, and the one experienced first, is **neuro-toxicity**. inhaled heavy-metal nanoparticles and unique mycotoxins cross the blood-brain barrier. the immediate effects are meth-like stimulation, agitation, paranoia, and hallucinations. small doses are survivable. larger and cumulative doses are not. as exposure accumulates, the body's metabolism deteriorates and neurological symptoms appear before the systemic ones. survivors below the spore line decline progressively in cognition and physical condition until death. the kahu call this descent **<a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-creeping-dark">the creeping dark</a>**.

the kahu treat anyone living below five thousand feet for any extended period as presumed peli-shen-affected and therefore impaired and unreliable. this is a working assumption. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/zou-esh">zou-esh</a> judgment of newcomers in the field is made about people who are, by default, neurologically compromised. detox-before-secrets is the protocol that follows. nothing of <span class="proper"><a class="wiki-link" href="/places/tora-vyl-drak">tora vyl drak</a></span> is shared with a newcomer until they have been brought above the line, observed in quarantine, and recovered to a state in which they can consent to what they are being told.

the kill line of approximately five thousand feet is not a wall. it is the elevation at which three environmental factors collectively defeat the fungus. **temperature**: as a mutated mesophile, peli-shen requires a warm post-impact baseline, and above five thousand feet cold ground temperatures and harsh seasonal freezes break the mycelial lifecycle and sporulation. **ultraviolet radiation**: the heavy-metal absorption traits of the fungus left it highly vulnerable to uv, and thinner high-altitude air lets through intense unfiltered uv that sterilizes the spores. **spore density**: because peli-shen spores bio-accumulate dense heavy metals, they are physically heavy, and in thin air they lack the atmospheric buoyancy to remain suspended at lethal concentration.

the caldera floor of <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span> sits at six thousand feet, one thousand feet above the kill line. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/nu-tera">nu-tera</a> plateau sits just above five thousand, a smaller margin.

the zou-esh operate below the line through a layered set of protections. the cloth mask, standard kit, filters peli-shen at concentrations encountered below five thousand feet. the kahu cat, which accompanies the zou-esh into the lowlands, handles peli-shen through a hybrid system of native biological resistance bred into the lineage over a millennium and pre-mission inoculation administered by <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-cat-kahu">the cat-kahu</a>. the cat protocol is the parallel to the zou-esh respiratory biome inoculation called <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/air-klin">air-klin</a>, applied before the mission and cleared from the body afterward.
