the kahu cats are the serval-cougar hybrid cats bred over approximately a thousand years by the cat-kahu, from the original lineage that arrived in <span class="proper">tora vyl drak</span> with mara's diplomatic mission during the age of bloom. they are the partners of the zou-esh in the field. they are the only large companion species that came through the dark century with humanity.

the original cats almost certainly descended from late-21st-century engineered pet biology. the pre-collapse world had developed extensive bioengineering capabilities for the companion animal market, and serval-cougar hybrid bloodlines were among the lines that survived the fracture in some pockets. the dark century selected from those bloodlines for the traits that made human partnership possible: stable temperament, working trainability, resilience to harsh conditions, and ability to bond across multiple human generations. the cats that arrived with the cat-farmers below the caldera were the survivors of that selection.

under cat-kahu stewardship across roughly a thousand years of deliberate breeding and kahu biotech support, the cats have continued to evolve. adult kahu cats run notably larger than the cougar baseline; mara's cat, at the time of mara's death in the age of bloom, was approximately one hundred and two pounds. modern adult cats run larger still in most lineages. lifespan has been extended to match the long working life of a zou-esh; under kahu biotech a kahu cat can live the working span of an active zou-esh career, and a single cat may know two human partners across its life.

the cats' partnership with the zou-esh is built across the years of zou-esh training. a student is paired with a cat early. the cat-kahu manage the pairing. the student and the cat develop trust, trained sequence, and working rhythm together. they deploy together when the zou-esh enters active mission service. they operate as a unit; neither the zou-esh nor the cat functions in the field as well alone. the trust between them is one of the deep institutional patterns of the order.

operationally in the field, the cats handle <span class="kt">peli-shen</span> through a hybrid system. they have native resistance bred into the lineage by the cat-kahu over centuries. and they receive pre-mission inoculation administered by the cat-kahu before each deployment, the parallel to the zou-esh's own air-klin respiratory inoculation. both protections are temporary and are cleared after the mission. the cat does not carry the lowlands' biology back into the caldera.

the cats are kahu in the broader sense in which the language uses the word. they are not human. they are part of the community, named, known, and remembered when they die. they are the closest non-human kin the kahu have. the kahu's regard for them is high by the standards of pre-collapse pet ownership and ordinary by the standards of working-animal partnership. the cats hold their place in the community through their function.
