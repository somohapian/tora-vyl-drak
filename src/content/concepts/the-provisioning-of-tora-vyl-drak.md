the caldera and plateau feed about two hundred seventy-eight thousand people with room to spare, and the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a> worry about food anyway. the <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/sun-tera">sun-tera</a>'s forty-some thousand acres out-produce the population's caloric need on their own; the <span class="kt">vyl-tapi</span> algae terraces produce twenty to fifty times conventional yield per acre; <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/hart-wata">hart-wata</a> carries the fishery; <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-hearth">the hearth</a>'s warm caves grow what sunless farming grows. the pressure is not calories. it is diversity, at six thousand feet with a short season; resilience, with <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-caldera-shudder">the shudder</a> in living memory and the reserves kept deep because of it; the specialty cultures and seed lines the biotech depends on, which are harder to replace than bulk food; and the herds of <a class="wiki-link" href="/places/the-wrap-around">the wrap-around</a>, which feed the <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/kaelith">kaelith</a>. the kahu are not hungry. they are careful, and they can tell you exactly why.
