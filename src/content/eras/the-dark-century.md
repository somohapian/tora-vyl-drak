the dark century is the era between <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-fracture">the fracture</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-first-descent">the first descent</a>. it spans approximately 2075 to 2120 ad, roughly forty-five years. it is the era before the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a>, the era during which the people who would become the kahu's ancestors survived underground, watched the surface, and waited.

the era opened with the impact day, the planetary fires of <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/the-hyper-winter">the hyper-winter</a>, and the global silence of the emp. within hours, half the world's population was dead. within years, ninety percent of the survivors had died from cold and starvation. the people who lived past that initial winnowing were the people who had been deep enough, prepared enough, lucky enough, or some combination. the kahu's ancestors, in the bunkers of the idaho rockies and the blue mountains of eastern oregon, were prepared and lucky and high enough to survive the secondary effects.

the dark century inside the bunkers was finite. the bunkers had not been designed as multi-generational arks. fuel reserves ran down. mechanical components failed without replacement parts. air-scrubber filters and water gaskets gave out and had to be cannibalized from non-essential sectors. within the first two decades, the engineering inheritance of the bunker generation was largely gone. within a generation, the social command structures of the original entry generation had also given way. the children of bunker-life were not the same people their bunker-builder ancestors had been.

food came from three sources across the long stretch. the ration, heavily guarded non-renewable pre-collapse stockpiles, reserved for the youngest and the sick. the farmed, fungiculture beds growing on recycled organic waste and small herds of hardy blind goat-like livestock bred to live underground on fungi. and the staple, <span class="kt"><a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/ston-shen">ston-shen</a></span>, the chemosynthetic lichen-bacteria paste grown on geothermal vents. ston-shen tasted strongly of sulfur and was the primary food source that sustained the survivors.

speech degraded across the dark century. formal english gave way to a simplified english-creole patois the speakers called tok-peles, the bastard tongue. complex tenses dropped out. vocabulary contracted. the language became operational, the language of bunker maintenance and shared work, less the language of pre-collapse civilization.

the long watch, across the decades after the caldera formed in 2075, was the period during which the bunker survivors monitored the cooling of the newly formed caldera from the surrounding peaks. they took elevation readings, measured atmospheric composition, observed weather patterns, and waited. by around 2112 the readings were good enough, and the surviving groups began to reach the basin. the dark century ended with the first descent and the founding, around 2120 ad.

the kahu treat the dark century as the period that shaped their later approach to limits. several elements of kahu culture trace to it: the practice of shared communal life, the philosophical commitment to <a class="wiki-link" href="/language/kahu"><span class="kt">kahu</span></a> as one word at every scale, the food economy's conservative reserves, and the distrust of technological brittleness.
