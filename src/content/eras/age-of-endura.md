the age of endura is the modern era of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/concepts/kahu">kahu</a>. it began in approximately 3925 ad, when the <a class="wiki-link" href="/eras/age-of-bloom">age of bloom</a>, the twelve-hundred-year golden age of kahu civilization, gave way to a longer and quieter time of strain. the kahu of the present, in 4625 ad, live seven hundred years into the age of endura.

the bloom did not end in catastrophe. it ended in the slow accumulation of questions the kahu's earlier institutions had not been built to answer. resource allocation strains, philosophical schisms taking root, the social structures of a population at carrying capacity for the kind of life it actually wanted. the kahu of the bloom inherited a sanctuary, a language, a council, a biotech tradition, and a stable food economy. the kahu of the endura inherit the same things, along with the unresolved problems those institutions had not addressed.

the central events of the age of endura, on the public record, are <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-caldera-shudder">the caldera shudder</a> of approximately 4525 ad and <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-schism">the schism</a> that the shudder accelerated. both are ongoing in their consequences. the geothermal grid has been repaired. the disagreement about the proper duration of a kahu life remains unresolved.

the name <span class="kt">endura</span> means endurance in kahu-tok. the kahu do not interpret the endura as decline. they interpret it as the period during which what the bloom built must be maintained, defended, and where necessary changed.

how long the age of endura will last, and what era will succeed it, is not in the record.
